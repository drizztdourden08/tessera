# Tessera v0.38.0

This release makes panels and menus behave the same everywhere: every panel that folds uses one button with a direction, side panels work on any page, and menu options show a faint dot that turns into a check or radio mark. It also brings widget kits their lighter frames, groups and snapping, lists that delete with an inline Undo, a help mark beside form labels, and fixes from Relic of the Past's testing.

## New

- CollapseToggle is the one fold button with a chevron that points where the panel goes. SideNav, SideNavLayout, SidePanel, StudioLayout and ListDetailLayout all use it, and every folded rail has the same look.
- SidePanel works on its own on any page, keeps its content while folded, and takes `rail` for what the folded rail shows.
- Field, FormRow, Checkbox and Toggle take `help`, a small question mark after the label that shows the note on hover, focus or click.
- Widgets take `chrome`: `full`, `compact` (a 21 px strip) or `none` (the buttons and a grip show on hover or focus).
- DockLayout keeps widget groups apart with `group` and `canDrop`, so a refused drop shows no hint, and `floatSnap` snaps floating widgets to edges with guide lines.
- ItemList takes `rowActions`, `undoDelete` (the row fades with an Undo before it is deleted) and `deleteKey`. `toast()` takes `onClose` with the reason it closed, and `useNameEdit` is public.

## Changes

- An option that is off in a menu shows a faint centred dot, which turns into a check or a filled radio mark when picked, and back; at once under reduced motion.
- The side menu's folded rail is 60 px wide, so its icons sit exactly centred.
- A hint closes when the pointer leaves, on blur, on Escape and on an outside click; a click shows it but never holds it open.

## Upgrading

- `PaneFoldButton` is gone, and `--side-nav-toggle-d`, `--side-nav-toggle-hit` and the `pane-fold-button` class are renamed; RENAMES.json maps them.
- The side menu's fold button is now named "Hide navigation" or "Show navigation".
- MIGRATION.md sections 245 to 251 describe each change.

## Fixes

- The unfilled part of a Slider track shows again on cards and panels.
- A disabled reason hint no longer stays stuck open after a click.
- Pressing the icon of a drag handle starts the drag.
