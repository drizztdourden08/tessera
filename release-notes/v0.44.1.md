# Tessera v0.44.1

A concealed or full screen title bar no longer flickers when the pointer moves across it.

## Upgrading

- MIGRATION.md section 268 describes the change; an app can drop its own workarounds for the flicker.

## Fixes

- A concealed or full screen WindowTitleBar is no longer a window drag area, so the page keeps the pointer while it moves over the bar. Once revealed, the bar stays while the pointer is over it or its open menus, and hides about 400 ms after it leaves both. Opening a menu no longer moves the page down.
