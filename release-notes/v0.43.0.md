# Tessera v0.43.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- Load state, save state and delete buttons pressed twice, with an icon that loops while it waits. The named set gains `load-state` and `save-state`: a platform seen from three quarters with an arrow rising out of it or coming down into it, in the 2 unit line of the Lucide icons. `ArmedIcon` draws them and `trash-2` in parts and, with `armed`, morphs into an armed pose and loops over `--duration-armed-loop` (1.4 s): the load arrow keeps lifting, the save arrow keeps sinking while the platform squashes and ripples, and the trash lid keeps lifting; only `transform` and `opacity` move, and reduced motion shows the armed pose still. `ArmedIconButton` is a ghost icon button on the shared confirm ask of ConfirmIconButton: the first press arms it, the second runs `onConfirm`, and a 4 second timeout, a blur or Escape disarms it; armed, its name and tooltip read Click again to and the label, `tone` colours the icon and `timeout={false}` waits for blur or Escape. The 4 second wait of the DropdownMenu confirm item is now the shared `CONFIRM_AGAIN_MS`.
- List rows start at the inset of a Button: one token, `--row-inset` (the Button inline padding, `--control-pad-md`, or `--control-pad-sm` in a compact scope), through one rule for ListItemRow, ItemList, CheckList, FileList, GroupTree and the options of Select and Combobox. The open Select, Combobox, DropdownMenu and ControlMenu read as one shape with their trigger, with no line at the join at any device scale, and the highlighted option fills the whole row. ListItemRow takes `media`, `mediaAspect`, `mediaWidth` and `nameAction`; ItemList takes `media` from `render` and `renameInline` to rename in place with a pen.
- ScreenWindow drops its title row with `titleRow={false}` and puts a small round X on the top right corner of the card, half over its edge, drawn by the same round button as CollapseToggle. The title still names the dialog, Escape still closes, and the X shows a tooltip with the Esc key. The window gets tighter: the gap around the card takes the next smaller space token, the padding is lg on the sides and bottom, and the content starts `--space-sm` (8 px) under the floating switch, 9 px measured with the card border. `movable` adds a lock left of the X, grey while unlocked and in the primary colour while locked, its shackle lifting and swinging open on transform and stroke only. Locked, the default, the window sits centred; unlocked, it moves by the top edge of the card, by drag or the arrow keys, and resizes from every edge and corner, inside the screen and down to `minSize`. Locking snaps it back to the centre and unlocking puts it back where it was. `storageKey` remembers the lock and the rect; `locked`, `defaultLocked`, `onLockChange`, `defaultRect` and `onRectChange` keep them in the app. ScreenLayer takes `corner`, `tight` and `move` for the same, and `ScreenLayerMove` is exported. DockLayout's floating resize edges are now `rect-resize` in place of `dock-resize`, shared with ScreenLayer.
- SidePanel is the one side panel standard. Its body sits on the sunken surface in place of the raised one. A line runs down its inner edge in every state, with the CollapseToggle on it, set in the row of the heading so it lines up whatever the heading height; `resizable` adds the grip and the drag, and `resizable={false}` keeps the line and the toggle. Folded, it is a 60 px sunken rail that shows the `actions` at its top level with the toggle, the title written sideways (reading up on a left panel and down on a right one), then the `rail` icons. A glow around a rail icon now spreads past the rail: only a rail too long to fit scrolls and clips. StudioLayout's panels follow, and ListDetailLayout's folded rails take the same sunken look and reading direction. The `side-panel--overhang` and `side-panel__frame` classes are gone, and `side-panel__line` is new.

## Fixes

- VolumeIcon in four steps, with waves that grow taller as the level rises. It draws one wave up to 25%, two up to 50%, three up to 75% and four above that, so 25, 50, 75 and 100% each look different; `VolumeStep` is now `0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4`. Every step makes the shown waves taller: each arc draws out from its middle toward its tips, to 60, 73, 87 and 100% of its length, and the change morphs over the same 390 ms on `stroke-dashoffset`, so the box never moves. The waves take `--c-volume-wave`, now set to `--c-primary` in place of `--c-primary-bright`; the speaker takes `--c-text`, white on the dark ground, in place of `currentcolor`; the X keeps `--c-danger`.
- WindowTitleBar's peek calls `preventDefault` on the Escape it handles, so the host does not act on the same key. A concealed bar now sits on its own layer, `--z-title-bar` (120), above the floating panes, without the host needing `isolation: isolate`.
