# Tessera v0.127.1

Two fixes asked for by Argus.

## Changes

- A click on a RecordHeader row that toggles now only folds or unfolds it. Inside a KanbanBoard it no longer calls onCardOpen; a click on the card body still does.
- ContentSwap removes the old layer after its computed animation time when animationend never fires, and at once when no animation is styled. A hidden tab or a test with no styles now shows the new content.
