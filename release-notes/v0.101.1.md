# Tessera v0.101.1

This release gathers everything since 0.95.0. It adds AnchoredBar, ZoomField, Avatar, TypeMark and several pixel art pieces, a CommandInput driven by patterns, DynamicInput paste and searchable choices, and windowed long lists. LogPanel filters fold to a slim rail, Wizard takes a stepper size, and Grid columns stay even with long content.

## New

- `AnchoredBar`, `TransientReadout`, `SpanBar` with `SpanLane`, and `ScrubRuler` are new composites that share one cell geometry. `AnchoredBar` takes `placement="auto"` and a `boundary` given as a ref, an element or a selector. `SpanBar` takes `tint` and `data`, a lane has one tab stop, and a read only bar does not activate. `ScrubRuler` takes `secondBand`, `markerAnchor`, `onCellContextMenu` and `compact`.
- `PixelThumbnail` draws a pixel picture crisp in a small box, with `box` sizes from 16 to 256 or `fluid`. `PixelPlayer` plays a pixel animation with a bar, ranges and keys, and its loop takes `pingpongReverse`. The icons `pixel-crop` and `pixel-trim` are new.
- `ZoomField` is a new composite: a zoom value in percent with step buttons, preset chips, an `actions` slot and a `slider` beside or below.
- `Navigator` takes `overlays`, `onOverlayPress`, `onWheelZoom` (with the wheel `deltaY`), `onKey`, `empty`, `caption`, `describe`, `unclamped` and `bigStep`. `step` and `bigStep` also take `{ x, y }`.
- `CommandInput` takes `commandSpecs`, a searchable picker of every command with a Send button named after the chosen one. A spec with `params` shows a field per param. Fields take `placeholder`, `dependsOn` and `options` as a function of the current values. A spec with `pattern` and `setup` renders one `DynamicInput` and sends its composed text, or `format(values)` when given.
- `DynamicInput` lists may be a function of the value. A choice the new list no longer offers is cleared. `patternText` is exported.
- `OptionGroup`, `OptionRow`, `ValueTag` and `OptionsPanel` lay out game settings: titled groups, rows with locked, forced and changed states and a reset, a read only value tag, and a tabbed panel with a side cell.
- `ResetButton` is a new composite: the reset icon with the name in its label and tooltip, shown only when a value has changed.
- `LogPanel` takes `filterPlacement` (top, left or right), `size` (md or sm) and `minRows`. A side filter column folds to a rail with the dashed edge and chevron, with `filtersCollapsed`, `defaultFiltersCollapsed`, `onFiltersCollapsedChange` and `storageKey`. `ToolFrame` takes `toolbarPlacement` and `shrink`.
- `VirtualList` takes row heights by index, `endGutter`, `renderOption`, `current`, `ownsKey`, `reorder` with `canDrop` and `dropLabel`, `scrollParent`, `initialFocusIndex` and `onPick`.
- `VirtualGrid` takes `density="swatch"`, `trailing`, `onWheelSize`, `ownsKey` and `tileProps(item, index)` for a class or data attributes on one tile. A swatch tile with `data-tile-look="dashed"` gets a dashed border.
- `RampStrip` takes `selected` with `onSelectionChange`, step `marks`, `onSwatchDragStart`, `swatchMenu`, `onStepClick`, `onStepDoubleClick`, `stepData`, `selectionKeys` and `wrap`.
- `Breadcrumbs` takes `trailingLabel`, a segment `title`, `opensMenu` and a sibling `group`, `dropTarget` and `validate`. A drag held over a segment with siblings opens its menu.
- `NumberField` takes `buttons` and `stepLabels`. `Slider` takes `scale="log"` and `ladder`. `Toggle`, `SegmentedControl` and `IconButton` take `mixed`.
- `useToggleSweep` takes `startWhen` and `expandStart`.
- `MarkdownEditor` takes `onImageFiles` and `count`. `MediaGrid` is a new composite and `FileInput` a new primitive.
- `Avatar` and `TypeMark` are new primitives, and `Text` takes `clamp`.
- `SignalStrip` folds a row of attention chips into a +N chip, and `OutcomeStrip` draws one toned cell per test step.
- The Argus mark is a faceted stone shield with eye cutouts and an emerald corner, drawn at three detail levels. `BrandMark` picks the small cut at `sm`, the medium cut at `md`, `h26` and `h39`, and the full cut above. `BrandMarkData.detail` holds the medium and small cuts.
- `BrandMark`, `Logo` and `Logo.Combined` take `twin`, which draws the emerald twin of Argus with the same facets. The tokens `--brand-argus-emerald`, `--brand-argus-stone-*` and `--brand-argus-twin-*` are new.
- A `DynamicInput` pattern slot may be optional: write a `?` after the type, such as `{seconds:number? 1..3600}`. It may stay empty, its placeholder is fainter, and `patternText` drops it with the space before it.
- A `CommandInput` pattern spec is ready with an optional slot empty. `format(values)` gets that slot as undefined, or an empty string for a text slot.
- `Breadcrumbs` serves pointer based drag and drop. `onSegmentRef(id, element)` hands out the element of each segment. `dropOverId` draws the drop look on a segment and runs the spring open hold for it. `springOpen` picks `menu` or `navigate` for what the hold does, and `onSpringOpen(id)` is called either way.
- `RampStrip` takes `onStepContextMenu(index, event)`, called on a right click or the menu key on a step. With `swatchMenu` as well, the handler runs first and the menu opens unless it calls `event.preventDefault()`.
- `SidePanel` takes `railSize`, `'default'` or `'slim'`. A slim folded rail is only the dashed line and its chevron, `--side-panel-rail-slim-w` wide, with no title, action or icons. `PaneRail` takes the same `size` in its `rail` prop.
- `TypeMark` takes `variant`, `'tile'` (the default) or `'outline'`. The outline shows only the icon in the category colour, with no tile, border or padding.
- `Avatar` takes `src`, a picture that fills the shape with `label` as alt text. If the picture fails to load, the initials of the label show instead.
- `Avatar` takes `shape`, `'circle'` (the default) or `'square'`. The types `AvatarShape` and `TypeMarkVariant` are exported.
- `IconButton` takes size `2xs`, a 16 px square with a 24 px hit area (token `--control-h-2xs`).
- `Text` takes `leading="16"` (token `--leading-16`), and `Icon` takes `hue`, which colours the glyph with a `Tag` category colour.
- The shared listbox of Select, Combobox and DynamicInput choice slots draws only the rows in view once a list holds more than 200 options. A list of thousands opens and scrolls at once, and the keyboard still reaches every row (I-95).
- DynamicInput takes a whole value pasted into any slot. A full code such as ABCD-1234 fills every slot at once (I-96).
- DynamicInput choice slots take `search`, a text box that filters the options as you type, and `free`, which keeps the typed text when no option is picked (I-97).
- DropdownMenu takes size `2xs` on a trigger, and KanbanColumn takes `header`, a node drawn in the column head (I-98).
- `TypeMark` gains `tone`: `info`, `success`, `warning` or `danger`. The tile takes the dim status colour with the icon in the status ink, and the outline shows the icon in the status ink. A tone wins over `color`, and the type `TypeMarkTone` is exported (I-100).
- `Wizard` and `WizardDialog` take `stepperSize`, `'sm'` or `'md'`, passed to their `Stepper`. With `'sm'` a vertical Stepper with sub-steps draws in its small look (I-103).

## Changes

- `useToggleSweep` swallows the click only when that press started a sweep.
- A pattern spec in `CommandInput` has no optional slots, so every slot must be filled before it sends.
- With `selectionKeys` false, the arrow, Home and End keys on `RampStrip` move focus between the steps without picking, and Enter or Space press the focused step.
- `CommandFieldsSpec` declares `setup?: undefined`, so `spec.setup` reads on every member of the `CommandSpec` union.
- `LogPanel` side filters fold to the slim strip.
- The lines beside the `LogPanel` filters keep a gutter of the new token `--pane-edge-overhang`, so the chevron no longer covers the time of a row.
- The `Toolbar` size type is now `ToolbarSize`, the `IconButton` sizes without `2xs`.
- A click on a choice chip opens its list, and a second click closes it. The list now sits under its chip (I-97).
- TypeMark outline fills the box the tile would take, 16 px at xs and 20 px at sm (I-98).
- `visibleRange` and `renderedIndices` now live in the data tier. They are still exported from the same places (I-95).

## Fixes

- The `DocumentTabs` overflow menu button sits on the center of the tab row.
- The `Widget` title icon sits on the center of the title text.
- `Widget` tab chips are an inline flex row. The icon sits level with the label, with a small gap between them.
- LogPanel scrolls inside a rigid ToolFrame. Its height is set by the frame and not by the rows (I-99).
- `Avatar` with a picture names itself once. The picture now has empty alt text and `label` stays on the avatar, so screen readers and tests read the name one time (I-101).
- `InlineEdit` shows its empty placeholder muted and in italic, so an empty field reads like the placeholder of a multiline description field (I-102).
- `Grid` with `columns` splits the row into equal tracks that long content cannot widen (I-104).
- `Select` and `Combobox` shrink inside a grid cell, and a long label ends in an ellipsis on one line (I-104).
