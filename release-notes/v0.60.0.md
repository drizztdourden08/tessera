# Tessera v0.60.0

This release places the controller marks where the real buttons are and lets a grid with one item fill its row.

## Changes

- Grid takes `fit` with `minColWidth`. Empty column tracks collapse, so a lone child or a short row fills the width instead of one column of it.

## Fixes

- ControllerSilhouette anchors and sizes follow the real Xbox Series, DualSense, Switch Pro and SNES pads, measured from front views scaled by each pad's published size. On the Xbox and DualSense layouts every control moves up, and the shoulders sit on the top edge. Anchor ids and outlines are unchanged, so marks placed from the anchors follow on their own.
