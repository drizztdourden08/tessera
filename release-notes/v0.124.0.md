# Tessera v0.124.0

RecordHeader joins the record family, and RecordCard fits inside a Kanban board with one frame.

## Changes

- RecordHeader is the title line of an item, with icon, code, badges, actions and a chevron. RecordCard, the Kanban group bar and Tree rows use it.
- KanbanBoard group headers fold their group in every column, through the fold argument of renderGroupHeader.
- RecordCard takes a name prop; its chevron reads Expand or Collapse followed by that name, or by aria-label.
- A folded RecordCard no longer renders its body, so the title is on the page once.
- RecordCard takes edge, edgeTone and variant and passes them to its Card.
- KanbanBoard takes cardFrame none to draw no frame of its own; renderCard gets the edge tone as its second argument.
