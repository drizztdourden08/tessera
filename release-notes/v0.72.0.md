# Tessera v0.72.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- KanbanBoard columns take `badge` after the count and `action` at the header end; a folded column hides the action and keeps the badge. New InlineEdit shows text that turns into a field on click, Enter or F2, with `validate`, `placeholder`, `disabled` and `multiline`. MarkdownEditor takes `onSubmit` on Mod+Enter, `onEscape`, and `maxLength` with a counter in the last 10 percent.
- LogPanel's `onInput` may return `false` or a promise (resolving `false`, or rejecting) to keep the typed text in the field, a second Enter is ignored while a promise is pending, and `inputDisabled` with `inputPlaceholder` shows a disabled input row.
