# Tessera v0.136.0

Tints and a quieter menu for record cards and the Kanban board.

## New

- `RecordCard` takes `tint`, a subtle tag colour mixed into its glass fill and border, on every density including compact.
- `KanbanBoard` takes `groupAccent`, a colour per group. That group's frame and bar take the hue, so an app can tint only its epic frames.
- `RecordHeader` and `RecordCard` take `menuOnHover`. The menu stays hidden until the card is hovered or focused, keeps its space so nothing shifts, and always shows on touch screens.
