# Tessera v0.56.0

This release completes toolbar docking: toolbars snap to any side of a work area, two per side, by mouse or keyboard, and a Toolbars menu shows and hides them. Options of segmented controls and toggle groups get tooltips with shortcuts, and pixel icons now always render crisp.

## New

- ToolbarDock holds toolbars on the four edges of a work area, up to two lanes per side. Top and bottom toolbars run horizontally; left and right toolbars run vertically. It hands each toolbar its orientation and a handle for its grip.
- Drag a toolbar by its grip to another lane or side. A preview shows where it lands, a full side refuses it, and dropping it on the basket hides it. Escape puts it back. Toolbars never float, and they never mix with widget docking.
- The keyboard does every move: Enter or Space on the grip picks the toolbar up, the arrow keys choose a side and then the outer or inner lane, Delete hides it, Enter drops and Escape cancels. Each step is announced.
- `useToolbarLayout` keeps the layout controlled. `toolbarMenuGroups` builds the Toolbars menu: one check item per toolbar and a Reset toolbars item.
- SegmentedControl and ToggleGroup options take a `tooltip` with a `description` and a `shortcut`, placed with `tooltipPlacement`. Icon-only options get a name on hover.

## Changes

- The tooltip body is the new `TooltipContent` primitive, shared by Toolbar and the option tooltips. The shortcut parser is `shortcutKeys` in Shortcut.
- WindowGuideOverlay takes an optional `title` and `showSnapping`. Its default look is unchanged.

## Fixes

- Pixel icons render at 24, 48 or 72 px. Icon snaps any other size to the nearest multiple of its 24 px grid, and IconButton and Button grow to hold a 24 px icon, so toolbar tools stay sharp.
