# Tessera v0.54.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- Bxel, the pixel art editor, joins the family as a brand. The `bxel` palette is new: import `@drizztdourden08/tessera/palettes/bxel.css` and set `data-palette="bxel"`. Violet is the primary colour, crimson the secondary, magenta the tertiary, with Night as the black and Mist as the white. `BrandApp` takes `bxel`, and `BRAND_FAMILY.bxel` carries its wide mark, its square icon, its wordmark, gradient and backdrop. The wide mark is the Merge 1 logo drawn as paths and stays for wide spaces. The square app icon is the crimson pixel B on a Night tile: `BrandInfo` has a new optional `iconMark`, and `BrandMark variant="app-icon"` and `pnpm icons` draw it in place of the mark. On the dark gradient the wide mark takes the light outline. The splash file draws a mark at least twice as wide as it is tall at half the canvas, and the static splash kit has `ts-mark--wide` for it. The icon files are written under `brand/bxel`, `brand/light-rim` and `brand/dark-ground`, and the Bxel gradient and backdrop tokens are in `brand.css`. Bxel appears in the brand pages and in the app switcher of the gallery. It has no mascot yet.
- ControllerLayout, StickPlot and PressedGrid are removed: the controller feature is a Brock plugin, and it builds its live parts there. Tessera keeps the plain parts: `ControllerSilhouette` with its paths, anchors and view box, `InputIcon` and the input icon sets. Build a live layout on those.
- New `FloatingPrompt` composite: a bar in the look of a Toast that floats at the foot of a region, asks a question with actions from data such as Apply and Cancel, and blocks that region until it is answered. It blocks its parent by default or the whole window with `scope="window"`, moves focus into the bar and back, and runs the action marked `cancel` on Escape.
- New `--c-focus` colour role, `var(--c-primary)` by default, used by every focus ring. New `--p-surface` seed, `var(--p-tertiary)` by default, that tints the surface, sunken and border ladder.
- Icon draws pixel art icons through the same `name` prop: `pixel-pencil`, `pixel-eraser` and the rest of the tool rail, drawn on a 24 by 24 grid in one colour. They stay crisp at 24, 48 and 72 px.
- WindowTitleBar takes `viewItems`, menu nodes the app adds to its View sub-menu after Tessera's own rows and a separator. With `view={false}` and `viewItems` set, the main menu still draws a View sub-menu holding only those entries.
- Adds the toolbar layout model for the coming ToolbarDock: a JSON-safe `ToolbarLayout` with at most two toolbars per side. Pure ops create, migrate, move, hide, show and toggle it: `createToolbarLayout`, `migrateToolbarLayout`, `moveToolbar`, `hideToolbar`, `showToolbar`, `toggleToolbar`, `isToolbarShown`, `canShowToolbar` and `toolbarOrientation`.
- New composite Toolbar. It is a row or a column of tools built from data: buttons, toggles, groups of choices, a number and separators, each with a hint that shows its name, description and shortcut. A tool with `options` opens a DropdownMenu on hover, below a row and beside a column, and items that do not fit fold into More. An optional `handle` draws a grip that a dock can wire through `handleProps`. Exports: `Toolbar` and its item types. DropdownMenu can open to the `right` or `left` of its anchor and flips when there is no room. It also takes `openOnHover` on a trigger, and `hover` and `focusOnOpen` on an anchored menu. Tooltip takes `right` and `left` as placements.

## Fixes

- ColorSwatch forwards its ref to its button, so an app can anchor a popover to it. Select draws `options` before `groups` when both are given, in place of dropping `options`. The ColorPicker usage says ColorPicker and ColorPickerPopover are imported from `/color-picker` and `/color-picker-popover`, which keep the colour library out of an app that never uses them.
- The composites barrel is split into grouped barrels for maintenance; the public exports are unchanged.
- The drop hints, drop preview, pop zone and drag ghost of DockLayout now live in a shared dock-zones folder, so other composites can use them. DockLayout users see no change.
- The guide takes a usage file for a hook module, a lowercase folder with an index that a tier index re-exports. It gets its own page and a Hooks section in the index, and `reorder` is the first.
