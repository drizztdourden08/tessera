# Tessera v0.54.0

This release brings the editor pieces drawing apps asked for: a Toolbar, menus that open to the side, a floating prompt, pixel art icons and the first part of toolbar docking. Bxel joins the family as a brand. The controller composites move to Brock's controller plugin, and Tessera keeps their plain parts. New colour roles let a palette set its own focus ring and surface tint.

## New

- Toolbar is a row or a column of tools built from data: buttons, toggles, groups of choices, a number and separators. Each tool has a hint with its name, description and shortcut. A tool with `options` opens a menu on hover, below a row and beside a column. Tools that do not fit fold into More. An optional `handle` draws a grip that a dock can wire through `handleProps`.
- FloatingPrompt is a bar in the look of a Toast at the foot of a region. It asks a question with actions such as Apply and Cancel and blocks that region until it is answered. `scope="window"` blocks the whole window. Focus moves into the bar and back, and Escape runs the action marked `cancel`.
- Pixel art icons work through the same Icon `name`: `pixel-pencil`, `pixel-eraser`, `pixel-fill-bucket` and the rest of the drawing tools. They are drawn on a 24 by 24 grid in one colour and stay crisp at 24, 48 and 72 px.
- The toolbar layout model for the coming ToolbarDock: a JSON-safe `ToolbarLayout` with at most two toolbars per side, and pure functions to create, migrate, move, hide, show and toggle it.
- The Bxel brand: the `bxel` palette (import `palettes/bxel.css` and set `data-palette="bxel"`), its wide mark, and its square app icon, a crimson pixel B on a Night tile. `BrandInfo` takes an optional `iconMark` for an app icon that differs from the mark.
- New colour roles: `--c-focus` (the primary colour by default) drives every focus ring, and the `--p-surface` seed tints the surface, sunken and border colours. Both leave every current palette unchanged.

## Changes

- DropdownMenu opens to the `right` or `left` of its anchor and flips when there is no room. A trigger can open on hover with `openOnHover`.
- Tooltip takes `right` and `left` as placements.
- WindowTitleBar takes `viewItems`, entries the app adds to its View menu after Tessera's own rows and a separator.
- A splash mark at least twice as wide as it is tall is drawn at half the canvas, and the static splash kit has `ts-mark--wide`.
- The guide gives a hook module its own page and a Hooks section. The reorder hooks are the first.

## Upgrading

- ControllerLayout, StickPlot and PressedGrid are removed. They live in Brock's controller plugin from Brock 0.82.0. Tessera keeps ControllerSilhouette with its paths, anchors and view box, InputIcon and the input icon sets. MIGRATION.md entry 274 has the details.

## Fixes

- ColorSwatch forwards its ref to its button, so a popover can anchor to it.
- Select given both `options` and `groups` draws the options first, then the groups, instead of dropping the options.
- The ColorPicker usage says to import ColorPicker and ColorPickerPopover from their own paths, which keeps the colour library out of apps that never use them.
- The drop hints, preview, pop zone and drag ghost of DockLayout are shared parts now, ready for the toolbar dock. DockLayout looks and behaves the same.
- The composites barrel is split into groups. The public exports are unchanged.
