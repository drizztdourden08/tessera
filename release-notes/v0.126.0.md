# Tessera v0.126.0

RecordHeader moves its content to the end of the row and drops the chevron's button look.

## Changes

- RecordHeader puts the content slot at the inline end, next to the menu; the title takes the free space.
- The chevron shows as a bare icon with no padding, through a new IconButton variant, bare. It stays a real button with aria-expanded.
- A click anywhere on a RecordHeader row with a chevron toggles it, except a click on the menu or another control.
- The check that skips clicks on controls now works for elements inside another frame, such as the catalogue preview.
