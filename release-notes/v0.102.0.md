# Tessera v0.102.0

This release adds the ChronoKi brand and its pixel font.

## New

- The `chronoki` palette is new, in light and dark. Its look is aged gold and oxblood around a clock. Set it with `data-palette="chronoki"`.
- The ChronoKi brand is new: an original clock mark in the family, its app icons for desktop and Android, its mark files and its splash. The files live in `brand/chronoki/` and the ground and rim variants beside it.
- The pixel font ChronoType by Caveras is available as `var(--font-chrono)`. Tessera adds four glyphs it lacked: `¼`, `½`, `¾` and `★`. The font stays under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, so it is for non-commercial use only and credits Caveras.
