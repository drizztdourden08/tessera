# Tessera v0.93.2

This patch makes small buttons and selectable chips big enough to click, without changing how they look.

## Fixes

- An extra small Button or IconButton and a selectable Tag now take a 24 px click area, the size the accessibility check asks for. Each still draws at 20 px, and its layout space is unchanged.
- Hover and pressed colours stay inside the drawn control, and button groups, split buttons and widget title bar buttons look exactly as before.
- Toolbars measure the drawn size, so they fold tools into More at the same width as before.
- In Windows high contrast, focus and hover still show the highlight colour.
- ActionTile tools and the end of DocumentTabs have a 4 px gap, so neighbouring click areas no longer overlap.
