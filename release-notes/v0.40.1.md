# Tessera v0.40.1

Every date Tessera writes now goes through `formatDate`, the one way to format a date or a time, and a check keeps it that way.

## Upgrading

- MIGRATION.md section 256 describes the change.

## Fixes

- A date shown in a Select or Combobox with no format of its own reads the same as the `date` format, for example "Oct 16, 2026", and follows the app's date locale, in place of the browser's short numeric date.
