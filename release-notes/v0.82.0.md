# Tessera v0.82.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- Status takes `dotSize="lg"` for an 8 px dot and `halo` for a soft ring in the tone around the dot. Tag takes `dot` to lead the label with a small dot in the tone of the tag. IconButton `tone` takes `info` and `accent`, beside `danger`. Card takes `header`, any content for the header row in place of the title, subtitle and actions. New TrackTable: a flat table whose columns take `fr` shares of the width and `drop` 1, 2 or 3 to hide under container queries as the table narrows. The breakpoints are the `--track-drop-1` to `--track-drop-3` tokens.
- Drawer takes `look="record"`: header chips show as written and the title is plain text colour in sentence case. KanbanBoard takes `cardEdgeTone(card)`, which draws a coloured edge on the cards it returns a tone for.
- DocumentTabs takes a `status` on a tab: a phase dot from the Status primitive before the title, with its label for screen readers. New SeatCard and SeatGrid lay out the seats of a lobby: a seat is filled, needs attention, is empty, or is a dashed button to add one.
- A widget and a PanelContainer take a `sublabel`: a muted label right after the title in the title strip. WidgetDefinition and WidgetTab take `sublabel`, which the title bar of a single widget or of the open tab draws; the tab chips of a tabbed pane never show it. The sublabel has its own ellipsis and shrinks first, so the title only shrinks once the sublabel is gone, and when either is clipped the full text shows in a Tooltip. Both share one title strip part, so the look is the same.
- WizardDialog takes a `size` (`md`, `wide`, `lg` or `xl`, from 640 to 1024 px; the default `wide` is the 768 px it always had). Wizard and WizardDialog take a `busy` that holds Back, Next, Finish and the step list and shows the step's busy hint while the app works on something async. `wizard.goTo(id, { force: true })` moves to a step past the usual checks, for when that work is done.
