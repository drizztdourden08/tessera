# Tessera v0.74.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- Drawer takes `size` (md, lg or xl) for a wider side panel, such as a focus mode; a top drawer keeps its full width.
- An intro step of GuidedTour now shows a primary Start the tour button and a ghost Skip button instead of Back and Next, translated through the new `tour.introStart` and `tour.introSkip` strings, and the mascot now stands outside the lit hole whenever a stand outside fits.
