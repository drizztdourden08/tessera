# Tessera v0.41.0

The volume control is reworked: its sound waves grow and morph with the level, a red X shows when it is silent, and it can open a small slider on hover, including as a title bar button.

## New

- VolumeIcon draws a speaker whose waves grow in steps as the level rises and morph between steps, with a red X at 0 or while muted. Its box never changes size, and it switches at once under reduced motion.
- VolumeControl takes `slider="popover"`: a click mutes, and hover or keyboard focus opens a small slider that the wheel and arrow keys also move. It closes the same way hints do.
- WindowTitleBar takes a volume action with `bar: 'volume'`, drawn as that control on the bar and as a Mute row plus a slider row in the main menu.
- DropdownMenu takes slider rows with `kind: 'slider'`, which keep the menu open while they change.
- Slider takes `orientation="vertical"`.

## Changes

- VolumeControl and Video draw the new icon. A muted VolumeControl no longer turns the whole button red; only the X is red.

## Upgrading

- The wave colour is the `--c-volume-wave` token, which follows each palette's bright primary tone.
- MIGRATION.md section 257 describes each change.
