# Tessera v0.96.0

This release adds the timeline pieces Bxel needs, pixel thumbnails and a pixel player, a command picker for CommandInput built on DynamicInput, the randomizer option rows, ZoomField, MediaGrid and FileInput, and Avatar and TypeMark. LogPanel can put its filters at the side and fold them away.

## New

- `AnchoredBar`, `TransientReadout`, `SpanBar` with `SpanLane`, and `ScrubRuler` are new composites that share one cell geometry. `AnchoredBar` takes `placement="auto"` and a `boundary` given as a ref, an element or a selector. `SpanBar` takes `tint` and `data`, a lane has one tab stop, and a read only bar does not activate. `ScrubRuler` takes `secondBand`, `markerAnchor`, `onCellContextMenu` and `compact`.
- `PixelThumbnail` draws a pixel picture crisp in a small box, with `box` sizes from 16 to 256 or `fluid`. `PixelPlayer` plays a pixel animation with a bar, ranges and keys, and its loop takes `pingpongReverse`. The icons `pixel-crop` and `pixel-trim` are new.
- `ZoomField` is a new composite: a zoom value in percent with step buttons, preset chips, an `actions` slot and a `slider` beside or below.
- `Navigator` takes `overlays`, `onOverlayPress`, `onWheelZoom` (with the wheel `deltaY`), `onKey`, `empty`, `caption`, `describe`, `unclamped` and `bigStep`. `step` and `bigStep` also take `{ x, y }`.
- `CommandInput` takes `commandSpecs`, a searchable picker of every command with a Send button named after the chosen one. A spec with `params` shows a field per param. Fields take `placeholder`, `dependsOn` and `options` as a function of the current values. A spec with `pattern` and `setup` renders one `DynamicInput` and sends its composed text, or `format(values)` when given.
- `DynamicInput` lists may be a function of the value. A choice the new list no longer offers is cleared. `patternText` is exported.
- `OptionGroup`, `OptionRow`, `ValueTag` and `OptionsPanel` lay out game settings: titled groups, rows with locked, forced and changed states and a reset, a read only value tag, and a tabbed panel with a side cell.
- `ResetButton` is a new composite: the reset icon with the name in its label and tooltip, shown only when a value has changed.
- `LogPanel` takes `filterPlacement` (top, left or right), `size` (md or sm) and `minRows`. A side filter column folds to a rail with the dashed edge and chevron, with `filtersCollapsed`, `defaultFiltersCollapsed`, `onFiltersCollapsedChange` and `storageKey`. `ToolFrame` takes `toolbarPlacement` and `shrink`.
- `VirtualList` takes row heights by index, `endGutter`, `renderOption`, `current`, `ownsKey`, `reorder` with `canDrop` and `dropLabel`, `scrollParent`, `initialFocusIndex` and `onPick`.
- `VirtualGrid` takes `density="swatch"`, `trailing`, `onWheelSize`, `ownsKey` and `tileProps(item, index)` for a class or data attributes on one tile. A swatch tile with `data-tile-look="dashed"` gets a dashed border.
- `RampStrip` takes `selected` with `onSelectionChange`, step `marks`, `onSwatchDragStart`, `swatchMenu`, `onStepClick`, `onStepDoubleClick`, `stepData`, `selectionKeys` and `wrap`.
- `Breadcrumbs` takes `trailingLabel`, a segment `title`, `opensMenu` and a sibling `group`, `dropTarget` and `validate`. A drag held over a segment with siblings opens its menu.
- `NumberField` takes `buttons` and `stepLabels`. `Slider` takes `scale="log"` and `ladder`. `Toggle`, `SegmentedControl` and `IconButton` take `mixed`.
- `useToggleSweep` takes `startWhen` and `expandStart`.
- `MarkdownEditor` takes `onImageFiles` and `count`. `MediaGrid` is a new composite and `FileInput` a new primitive.
- `Avatar` and `TypeMark` are new primitives, and `Text` takes `clamp`.
- `SignalStrip` folds a row of attention chips into a +N chip, and `OutcomeStrip` draws one toned cell per test step.

## Changes

- `useToggleSweep` swallows the click only when that press started a sweep.
- A pattern spec in `CommandInput` has no optional slots, so every slot must be filled before it sends.

## Fixes

- The `DocumentTabs` overflow menu button sits on the center of the tab row.
- The `Widget` title icon sits on the center of the title text.
