# Tessera v0.106.0

New pieces for the Argus board and a heavier outline on the ChronoKi icon.

## Changes

- SegmentedBar is a new primitive that draws progress as equal segments, each with its own tone. It acts as a button when given `onClick`.
- KanbanBoard fires `onCardOpen(cardId)` when a card is clicked, but not after a drag or on a button inside the card.
- KanbanBoard puts cards with the same `group` inside one frame, with an optional header from `renderGroupHeader`. A move now reports the card ids above and below.
- SignalStrip takes `iconOnly` on the strip or a chip, a second mark (`mark2`, an icon or an avatar) and `wrap`.
- BrandInfo `iconOutline` is now an object with `colour`, `ratio` and `minPx`. ChronoKi uses a black outline of 6 percent of the icon size, never under 2 px. The other brands are unchanged.
