# Tessera v0.42.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- LogPanel kinds in any tone, and a count that hides at 0. A `LogKindDef` takes `tone?: LogKindTone`, a text tone, a tag tint such as `teal` or a hex colour from app data, `toneMessage?: boolean | LogKindTone` for a message tone of its own, and `uppercase?: boolean` for messages in capitals. A hex colour under 4.5:1 on the dark panel is lifted toward white until it reads, through the hex helpers Tag now shares from `src/primitives/hex-ink`. Badge takes `hideZero` on a count, and Tabs, Card, SectionHeader and HeaderAnchorNav take it and pass it to their counts, so a count of 0 draws nothing. A kind with a text tone or none, and a count without `hideZero`, keep their look.
- A sharp pixel art wordmark on the splash, and a wide wordmark with no empty band. Splash takes `wordmarkPixelated?: boolean | 'auto'` (`ImagePixelated`, the same option as Image, `false` by default): with `true`, or with `'auto'` once a picture of 256 px or less on its longest side has loaded, the wordmark takes `ts-wordmark--pixelated`, a new class of `splash.css` that scales it up to the 384 px row with `image-rendering: pixelated`, its height following its proportions up to 48 px. `ts-wordmark` now keeps the height of the picture up to 48 px (`block-size: auto; max-block-size: var(--size-48)`) in place of a fixed 48 px box, so a wordmark that reaches the 384 px cap draws its box only as high as the picture, with no empty band above and below.

## Fixes

- A docked pane saved without `makeRoom` makes room. `migrateLayout` sets `makeRoom: true` on such panes, and DockLayout and `mainRectOf` read a missing `makeRoom` as true, so the main view no longer runs under the panes of an older saved layout. A pane with `makeRoom: false` still overlays, and an overlay pane now widens the main view only on its own side, never over a pane that makes room.
