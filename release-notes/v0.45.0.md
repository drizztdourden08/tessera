# Tessera v0.45.0

Every pane that folds or resizes now has one edge: side panels, the studio layout, the list and detail layout and split panes all draw the same line, fold button and folded rail.

## New

- PaneEdge draws every pane edge the same way: a thin line with the round fold button beside it, not on it. Dragging a pane past its minimum folds it to the rail, and dragging the rail back out opens it. The arrow keys and Enter do the same, and a double click resets the width.
- ResizeHandle takes `fold` for that folding, and `HandleFold` and `FoldSide` are exported.

## Changes

- Every pane resizes: SidePanel and ListDetailLayout no longer take `resizable`. To keep a fixed width, give the same number to `minWidth` and `maxWidth`.
- Hovering or dragging a pane edge brightens the neutral line slightly, with no gold glow.
- DockLayout's divider and the DataTable column line show the same thin line at all times.

## Upgrading

- Removed: `SidePanel.resizable`, `ListDetailLayout.resizable`, `ListDetail.resizable`, ResizeHandle's `look`, `onCollapse`, `children` and `onClick`, the `ResizeHandleLook` export and the `--resize-handle-bar` token. `SplitPane.snapAt` is now `collapsible`. RENAMES.json maps each of them.
- MIGRATION.md section 269 lists what an app changes.
