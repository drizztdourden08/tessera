# Tessera v0.37.3

With the title bar's View sub-menu hidden, its pin and full screen buttons stay reachable on a narrow window, and those two menu items no longer show a check mark while they are off.

## New

- WindowTitleBar takes `onFitChange`, which reports the ids of the buttons the bar has tucked away whenever that list changes.

## Upgrading

- MIGRATION.md section 243 describes both changes.

## Fixes

- With `view={false}`, a pin or full screen button the bar tucks away on a narrow window is listed in the main menu, so it can still be reached.
- Pin and full screen in the title bar menu are plain actions whose words and icon say what a pick does, such as Unpin window or Exit fullscreen, in place of a check mark that showed even when they were off.
