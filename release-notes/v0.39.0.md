# Tessera v0.39.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- DateText shows a date or a time in any common format: `relative` (5 minutes ago), `calendar` (Today, 21:40), `date`, `time`, `datetime`, `weekday`, `month` and `iso`, each in a short, medium or long `length`, or Intl date options of your own. It renders a `time` element, shows the full date in a Tooltip (on by default for relative and calendar), and keeps relative and calendar text current from one shared clock. `formatDate(value, format, options)` gives the same text outside React. The locale and the words Just now, Today, Yesterday and Tomorrow come from the new `dates` strings. DataTable takes `SchemaConfig.dates` to format a column, and Select and Combobox columns take every date preset as `format`.
- A search box, a Select or a Combobox fits in a toolbar, and StudioLayout stacks only when its columns do not fit. TextInput, SearchInput, Select, NativeSelect and Combobox take `grow={false}`: the control keeps the set width `--field-w-inline` (256 px), shrinks to `--field-w-inline-min` (160 px) and sits on one line with the other tools in a ToolFrame toolbar or ToolGroups. StudioLayout stacks its columns when the open side panels, the folded rails and `editorMinWidth` (192 px when left out) do not fit side by side, in place of a fixed 768 px of its own width, so a studio beside a ListDetailLayout list keeps its columns and the editor its full height on an ordinary window.
