# Tessera v0.30.1

Keyboard users stay in control after a confirm question and in the command palette, menus near the right edge of a window keep their full width, and the guide handles views that several apps share.

## Changes

- DropdownMenu with a button takes `align`: `start` by default, or `end` or `auto` to open on the side with room.
- When a confirm question disappears because its row goes away, `onCancel` now runs once, so an app that keeps track of the question stays in step.

## Upgrading

- If your `onCancel` touches the part that asked, check the part is still on screen first, since it can now run after the part is gone.
- MIGRATION.md sections 215 to 218 describe each change.

## Fixes

- In the command palette, Escape now cancels a row's confirm question first, then clears the search text, then closes the palette, wherever focus is.
- After a confirm question ends, by the check or the cross, focus goes back to the button that asked, so the arrow keys and typing keep working in the command palette.
- The profile menu in SiteHeader, the SiteHeader link menu and every title bar dropdown open under the end of their button when it sits near the right edge, and keep their full width instead of being squeezed.
- The guide counts a views folder once when the project and several apps all list it, and it now reports two different folders that hold a part with the same name.
