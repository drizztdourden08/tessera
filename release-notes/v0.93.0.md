# Tessera v0.93.0

This release calms the slim scrollbar, answers more of Bxel's asks for its tree, toolbar and timeline, and fixes squeezed number fields in toolbars.

## New

- ScrollArea takes `thumb="auto"` on a slim scrollbar: the thumb hides at rest and shows on hover, while scrolling and while focus is inside.
- Tree takes `trail="hover"`, which shows a row's actions only on hover, focus or selection, and `onDragOut`, which drags a row or the picked set out of the tree. With `onDragOut`, Alt and drag reorders.
- SplitButton takes `panel`, so its chevron opens control rows such as a slider and toggles beside a pressed main button.

## Changes

- The slim scrollbar thumb rests in a quiet grey in every app and turns the primary colour on hover or drag.

## Fixes

- A Toolbar number item with `showLabel` keeps room for its value, and a bar too narrow for its items folds them into More instead of squeezing them.
