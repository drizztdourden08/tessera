# Tessera v0.93.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- ScrollArea takes `thumb`. With `thumb="auto"` on a slim area the thumb is hidden at rest and shows under the pointer, while the area scrolls and for a moment after (the new `--duration-scrollbar-idle` token), and while focus is inside it. It stays draggable with the same hit area, and reduced motion only shows and hides it. The default `always` keeps today's look. A slim area with nothing to scroll draws no thumb, and a test now covers it.
- The slim ScrollArea thumb now rests in `--c-border-strong`, a quiet grey, in every app, and turns `--c-primary` only under the pointer and while dragged.
- `SplitButton` takes `panel`, a render function that opens control rows behind the chevron in place of the `items` menu. Fill it with `ControlMenuRow` rows holding sliders, segmented choices and toggles, such as an onion skin toggle with opacity, frame range and tint. The panel sits under the chevron like the menu, takes focus on its first control, stays open while the controls change, and closes on a click outside or Escape, which returns focus to the chevron. The render function receives `close` for a control that should end the visit. The chevron then reports `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. `items` is now optional, and a `SplitButton` with `items` behaves as before.
- `Tree` takes `trail="hover"`, which fades the trail slot out at rest and shows it when the row is hovered, focused, holds focus inside it or is picked, so the row does not shift and Tab or an arrow into the row reveals the actions. It also takes `onDragOut(keys, event)`, which makes the whole row a native drag source for other widgets, with the count ghost of `useNativeDragGhost`. With `reorder` too, a plain drag leaves the tree and Alt with the drag reorders, as Alt with the arrows does. A new type, `TreeTrailShow`, is exported.

## Fixes

- A `Toolbar` number item with `showLabel` no longer squeezes its field to a sliver. The field was fixed at 96 px with the label inside it, so a long label left no room for the value. A labelled number now sizes to its label plus a field wide enough for its widest value, taken from `max`, `min` and `step`. The fit measure counts that width, and re-measures when `showLabel`, a bound, `step` or `suffix` changes, so a short bar folds whole items into More. A number without `showLabel` is unchanged.
