# Tessera v0.56.1

This patch fixes the seam of a list and detail layout whose two panes can both fold.

## Fixes

- ListDetailLayout with both panes collapsible now spaces its two seam buttons apart, each on its own pane edge, with its chevron pointing at the pane it folds. The detail draws the same dashed fold line as the list.
- A folded detail no longer hides half of its unfold button behind the rail.
