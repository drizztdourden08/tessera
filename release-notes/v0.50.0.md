# Tessera v0.50.0

Tessera can now draw a controller test screen. `ControllerLayout` shows a plain outline of a controller with every input in its place and lights each one live, so an app can show a pad being used without brand art.

## New

- `ControllerLayout` draws a plain outline of a controller with every input in its place, like a console test screen.
- `variant` picks the body: `offset` for Xbox shapes, `symmetric` for PlayStation shapes with the sticks side by side, `nintendo` for the offset body with face labels from the caller, and `pad` for a pad with no sticks or triggers.
- `slots` takes a state per input by its place, such as `face.south`, `dpad.up`, `trigger.left` or `stick.right.click`. `CONTROLLER_SLOT_IDS` lists them, and `ControllerSlotId` and `ControllerSlotState` type them.
- A held button lights in the primary colour. Triggers and shoulders fill with `value` from 0 to 1. A stick cap moves with `x` and `y` from -1 to 1, with `y` up, and a stick click lights the cap.
- A slot the variant has no place for, and every entry of `extras`, shows in a list under the outline, one row each.
- The outline grows with its card up to `--controller-layout-max-w` (640 px). Screen readers hear the figure name and a hidden list of what is held now, so a moving stick announces nothing.
- A new strings group, `controller`, holds the names of every input.

## Changes

- The composites barrel and catalogue are split into two files each. Every export stays the same, and `tessera new --group` finds a group in either catalogue file.
