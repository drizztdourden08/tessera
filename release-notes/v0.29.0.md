# Tessera v0.29.0

Escape now closes only the surface on top, whatever is open on the page, and the fixes and features Relic of the Past built on its own copy of the design system are part of Tessera, so an app gets them from the package.

## New

- One Escape stack serves every dialog, drawer, screen, menu, popover, guided tour, confirm question and drag: Escape closes the innermost one and leaves the rest open.
- `useEscapeStack` lets an app put its own layer on that stack, or ask what is open before it acts on Escape.
- A field's options can give each value a name, such as `{ value: 2, label: 'Hard' }`: the editor, the filter, the filter chip and the table cell show the name, and a number is saved as a number.
- A choice field shows its options as segments or chips while they fit the row, and becomes a searchable Select when they do not.
- DropZone accepts media types such as `image/*`, shows a success or error line, and takes files pasted with Ctrl+V.
- CompactRecordView takes `fieldRenderers`, to draw chosen fields with your own component.
- A reference field in RecordEditor and CreateRecordDialog can narrow its choices by another field of the same record.
- `SpaceToken` gains `2xs`, the smallest gap, for Flex, Stack, Inline, Center, Grid, ButtonRow and Spacer.

## Changes

- ScreenLayer takes `onClose`, and ScreenWindow passes its own, so every screen closes on Escape once nothing is open above it.
- A searchable Select clears its search text on the first Escape and closes on the second.
- A DropdownMenu closes on Escape even when focus has left it.
- CompactRecordView draws its group headings in the secondary accent colour.
- A number field written in hex shows its hex text as a hint under the number box.

## Upgrading

- DialogShell no longer catches Escape on its own. A control inside a dialog or screen that handles Escape itself, such as a search box clearing its text, calls `preventDefault` so the surface around it stays open.
- Sections 210 and 211 of `MIGRATION.md` describe each change and what an app does about it.

## Fixes

- An inline Field lets its control shrink, so two number boxes in a narrow column stay inside it.
- A colour picker that opens above its swatch no longer covers the swatch.
- Checkbox and DropZone show a red edge when their Field has an error.
- Grid `minColWidth` lets a single cell fit a box narrower than that width.
- A Button with a plain text label stays on one line and keeps its width.
