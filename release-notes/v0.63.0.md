# Tessera v0.63.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- ItemCard in compact density shows its tags on a line of their own, cut with a +N tag when they do not fit. ItemCard details take text or nodes, such as a Tooltip around a status, and stay on one line in compact. ActionItem takes loading, which shows a spinner and blocks the action, and nameSuffix, which makes the name and tooltip of each row unique. ItemCard takes mediaLabel to name a media that means something, and mediaTone accepts tertiary. ActionItem confirm takes false to skip the inline ask of a danger action, and true to make any action ask.
- `VirtualGrid` gives `getRects()` on its ref, the rects of every tile worked out from the layout, so a marquee can pick tiles that are not drawn; a section also takes `actions` (icon buttons at the end of its header) and `depth` (indent for nested folders).

## Fixes

- MIGRATION and RENAMES point past Brock 0.96.0, which retired CalibrationPanel and its own ControllerLayout: calibration is in the Controllers hub, and a pad is drawn with ControllerSilhouette or PadOverlay.
- DocumentTabs now draws a tablist whose direct children are only the tabs, with the close buttons in a layer after it that sits over each tab's end, so `[role=tablist] > [role=tab]` lookups work and aria-owns is gone. A Stepper step now names itself with exactly its visible text, the digit first, and says done or needs attention through a described note instead of in the name.
