# Tessera v0.107.0

Two more pieces for the Argus board.

## Changes

- SignalStrip `mark2` can show a brand mark with `{ brand }`, beside the icon and avatar forms. An avatar with `src` shows a plain image.
- SignalStrip chip `count` takes a short string such as `42h` or a number.
- Card takes `onActivate`. It fires on a click on the card, or on Enter or Space when the card has focus. Inner buttons, links, fields, menus and anything rendered in a portal never fire it.
- The check for an inner control now lives in `primitives/dom` and KanbanBoard uses it too, so Kanban cards also ignore clicks inside editable text.
