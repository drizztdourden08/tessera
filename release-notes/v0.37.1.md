# Tessera v0.37.1

Two small additions for apps built on Tessera: a splash can show the app's wordmark image in place of its name, and menu, nav and palette items report when they are highlighted, so an app can start loading a screen before it is picked.

## New

- `Splash` takes `wordmark`, an image URL drawn in place of the title text, and the static splash kit has a matching `ts-wordmark` class: 48 px high, at most 384 px wide and narrower in a narrow window, centred. The title stays the image's text for screen readers.
- DropdownMenu items, including the title bar menus, SideNav entries, FloatingSwitch items and CommandPalette rows take `onHighlight`. It is called once each time the item is hovered, focused or reached with the arrow keys, and never while it is disabled. Nothing looks different.
