# Tessera v0.135.0

Pieces for the Argus board: a drop slot, glass Kanban layers, steady card headers and two Stepper states.

## New

- `DropSlot` takes one dragged item. It shows a dashed empty state with its label, highlights on drag over, gives the payload to `onDrop`, and offers a clear button. Enter or a click calls `onPick`, so a menu can choose instead. `accept` limits the drag type.
- `KanbanBoard` takes `cardTint`, a subtle tint per card. The `renderCard` frame argument carries `tint`.
- `Stepper` has a not-started state: pass a null or missing `currentId`. The compact label reads "Not started".

## Changed

- `KanbanBoard` lanes, group frames and cards are tinted glass. The group frame borders run unbroken and the group bar spans the full frame width.
- `RecordHeader` slots share one height token, `--record-header-slot-height`. The content slot keeps the header gap and never clips an avatar ring.
- `RecordCard` compact wraps its title, drops the tickers and grows from a minimum height.

## Fixed

- `Stepper` with `complete` no longer reads "Step 4 of 3" in the compact label; it reads "Complete".
