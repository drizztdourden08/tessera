# Tessera v0.89.1

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Fixes

- DockLayout column corner handles are 24 by 24 px, up from 24 by 8 px, so they pass the axe target-size rule. The hit area sits 8 px above the seam and reaches 32 px up into the panel above it, so the widget title bar buttons below the seam stay uncovered and fully clickable. The seam centre is 12 px from the hit area, which keeps the seam divider inside the axe spacing exception. The drawn line stays on the seam and looks the same. Row corner handles are unchanged. Tests pin the 24 by 24 size, the 8 px lift and the 12 px clearance.
