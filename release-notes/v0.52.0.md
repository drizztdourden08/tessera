# Tessera v0.52.0

A blank controller outline for apps to draw on, and the end of every light variant. `ControllerSilhouette` draws only the outline of a controller, so the app places its own glyphs, labels and leader lines. No app in the family has a light theme, so everything made for a light background is gone.

## New

- `ControllerSilhouette` draws the outline of a controller: one closed, stroked line with no fill, no shading and nothing for a button, stick or trigger. `variant` picks `offset` (Xbox shapes), `symmetric` (PlayStation shapes), `nintendo` (Switch Pro shapes) or `pad` (no sticks or triggers).
- `children` render inside the SVG on top of the outline. The viewBox is fixed at `0 0 400 260` for every variant (`CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_VIEW_BOX`), and the overflow is visible.
- `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_ANCHORS` gives where each control sits per variant in viewBox units, with a radius for round controls. `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_PATHS` gives each outline's path for an app that draws it inside its own SVG.

## Upgrading

- BrandMark, Logo and Logo.Combined lose their `ground` prop. Inside Splash a mark still takes its dark look by itself.
- The `rim` prop, BrandRim and BrandRimTone lose the value `'dark'`, so a rim is `'none'` or `'light'`. The token `--brand-rim-dark` and the class `.brand-rim--dark` are removed.
- The light gradient pair is removed from every palette: `--p-gradient-light-from`, `--p-gradient-light-to`, `--c-gradient-light-from`, `--c-gradient-light-to`, and `gradientLightFrom` and `gradientLightTo` in `tokens.json` and `splash-tokens.css`.
- The folders `brand/light-ground` and `brand/dark-rim` are deleted. `brand/dark-ground` and `brand/light-rim` stay as they were. See MIGRATION.md section 271.

## Under the hood

- Outline sources. The `offset` outline is traced from "Xbox Series Controller Gold Shadow Special Edition" by Serhio Magpie, Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: silhouette traced, mirrored into one symmetric half and refitted as curves.
- The `symmetric` outline is traced from "Dualshock 4 Layout.svg" by Tokyoship, Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Changes: outer silhouette only, mirrored into one symmetric half and refitted as curves.
- The `nintendo` outline is drawn by hand from the proportions of a CC0 photo by Tim Whitehouse, and the `pad` outline is traced from a public domain photo by Evan-Amos, both on Wikimedia Commons.
