# Tessera v0.52.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- `ControllerSilhouette` draws the outline of a controller for the app to draw on. It is one closed, stroked line with no fill, no shading and nothing drawn for a button, stick or trigger. Shoulders show only where they stick out past the body, as on a real pad seen from the front. `variant` picks the outline: `offset` for Xbox shapes, `symmetric` for PlayStation shapes, `nintendo` for Switch Pro shapes and `pad` for a pad with no sticks or triggers. Each outline is symmetric left to right and follows the proportions of the real pad. `children` render inside the SVG on top of the outline, so the app places its own glyphs, labels and leader lines. The viewBox is fixed at `0 0 400 260` for every variant and is exported as `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_VIEW_BOX`. `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_ANCHORS` gives where each control sits per variant in viewBox units, such as `stick.left`, `dpad`, `face`, `shoulder.right` and `meta.guide`, with a radius for the round controls. Triggers sit above their shoulder, outside the outline. `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_PATHS` gives the SVG path `d` of each variant's outline, the same string the component draws, for an app that draws the outline inside its own SVG. The SVG overflow is visible, so children may draw past the box. - No app in the family has a light theme, so every variant made for a light background is removed. BrandMark, Logo and Logo.Combined lose their `ground` prop; inside Splash a mark still takes its dark look by itself. The `rim` prop and BrandRim and BrandRimTone lose the value `'dark'`, so a rim is `'none'` or `'light'`. The token `--brand-rim-dark` and the class `.brand-rim--dark` are removed. The light gradient pair, `--p-gradient-light-from`, `--p-gradient-light-to`, `--c-gradient-light-from` and `--c-gradient-light-to`, is removed from every palette, and `gradientLightFrom` and `gradientLightTo` from `tokens.json` and `splash-tokens.css`. The folders `brand/light-ground` and `brand/dark-rim` are deleted; `brand/dark-ground` and `brand/light-rim` stay as they were. The gallery drops its light cells and options, and its demo menus no longer list a Light theme. See MIGRATION.md section 271 for how an app upgrades.
