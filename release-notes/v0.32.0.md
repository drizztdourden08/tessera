# Tessera v0.32.0

This release brings in the small parts Relic of the Past kept its own copies of while it moved onto Tessera, so any app can now use them straight from the package.

## New

- Fifteen more icons: shuffle, database, store, camera, joystick, smartphone, wrench, dices, list-checks, scroll-text, network, person-standing, languages, music and layout-dashboard.
- A tab can be disabled with a reason, which shows on hover and is read to screen readers; the arrow keys skip it.
- Disclosure can be opened and closed by the app with `open` and `onOpenChange`.
- ItemCard takes `navigate` for app routing, and `mediaHeight="auto"` for a picture taller than its usual band.
- Tag takes any colour from your data with `variant="custom"`, and picks black or white text so it stays readable.
- A menu item icon can be a name, iconify data, Icon path data or an element.
- Anchored closes on Escape or a press outside, through `onDismiss`, and `useDismissListeners` is exported for your own popups.
- Tooltip takes `follow="pointer"` to sit beside the pointer and stay inside the window.
- A StackedBar segment takes a `description`, shown in its hover text.
- Shortcut can show a mouse drag with `mouse="drag"`.
- ListDetailLayout can size its columns by share with `listRatio`, and its detail side can fold to a rail like the list.
- LogPanel rows can be selected with a click, Shift and a click, or the arrow keys, for the app to copy or act on.
- `blankValue`, `IDENTITY_PATH` and `createScrollSyncController` are now public.

## Upgrading

- `TagVariant` gains `custom`, so a switch that lists every variant needs a case for it.
- MIGRATION.md sections 221 to 223 describe each change.
