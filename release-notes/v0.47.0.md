# Tessera v0.47.0

Stepper can now draw an editable ladder: a row of named steps with a picture above each circle, where any steps can be lit and a click toggles one on or off. An app no longer needs to reach into Stepper's internals to do it.

## New

- A step takes its own `state`, so steps 1, 3 and 4 can be lit while 2 stays off. The line into a step follows that step's state.
- A step takes `media`, a picture shown above its circle, and `struck` to cross its name out.
- `selectMode="toggle"` makes every step a button with `aria-pressed`, so a screen reader hears which steps are kept. `currentId` is optional in this mode.
- `labelPlacement="above"` puts the name over the picture and circle, and `labelLines` keeps room for wrapped names so the circles line up.
- `dotVariant="ring"` draws a coloured ring with the number inside and no fill.
- `stepWidth` and `mediaSize` pick the width of a step and the room for its picture, and `scroll` lets a wide row scroll sideways instead of overflowing.
- A `neutral` tone draws a grey ring, number and line.
