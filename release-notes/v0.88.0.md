# Tessera v0.88.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- Four new composites and six opt-in options for a project browser and a canvas. Breadcrumbs shows the levels of a path as buttons with icons, a menu of the places next to each segment, a fold of the middle segments behind an ellipsis through `maxVisible`, a drop target per segment through `onDropOn`, a trailing slot, and a PathInput that opens on a click at the end through `onPathSubmit`. Tile is a square media slot with a name line, four corner slots and separate `selected` and `focused` looks. SplitButton joins a main action to a chevron menu and takes `pressed` to toggle. Navigator is a small map of a large surface with a frame the user drags or moves by arrow keys, read aloud as a percent of the surface. FilterBar takes `maxChips`, which folds the extra chips into a +N menu where each can be removed, and `parseToken`, which turns a typed `key:value` word into a chip. Anchored takes `anchorRect`, a rect or a function that reads one, and `boundary`, which keeps the popup inside a scroll box. SplitPane takes `summary` and `header` for a collapsed stacked pane. AnchorPicker takes `value="custom"` for a free point and `size="xs"`. Image takes `backdrop="checker"` and `fit="integer"`. ContentHeader gains a usage example for the header of one subject.
- Colour, swatches and the choice grid. `ColorPicker` takes `modes` to show HSV, OKLCH and RGB tabs over one value, with `mode` and `onModeChange` to control the tab. The OKLCH tab clamps to sRGB by lowering chroma and shows a notice while the typed colour is outside the range. It also takes a `name` slot under the title and a `footer` slot under the buttons, and `ColorPickerPopover` passes them on. `ColorSwatch` takes `marks` for up to four corner marks, `unused` for a dashed struck look, `mixed` for a dash over the colour, and `focusRing` set to `apart` so the focus ring sits outside the selected ring. The new `ChoiceGrid` is a row or grid of any cells picked as one radio group or listbox, with one Tab stop, arrows, Home and End, sections, `columns`, `renderCell`, a `footer` slot and `pickOnRelease`. A RampStrip recipe in its usage file builds from it. Thirty nine pixel icons join the set: eight for the canvas views, thirteen for drawing and painting, and eighteen for selecting, transforming, scaling, zooming and the tile map.
- A disabled item in DropdownMenu, ContextMenu and every menu built on them is now drawn at the disabled opacity, the same as a disabled Button, so the whole item reads as unavailable instead of only a slightly dimmer label. ColorSwatch takes `fill`, which stretches it to the width of its grid cell and keeps it square; set `--swatch-aspect` on the cell for another shape.
- Number fields, a Mixed state and new Toolbar item kinds, all opt-in. `NumberField` scrubs by dragging its label, steps with the wheel and the arrow keys, takes a fine step with Shift, walks a `ladder` of stops and offers `presets` and a `suffix`; a zoom field is a ladder from 25 to 12800 percent. `AngleField` adds a dial and snaps to 15, 22.5 or 45 degrees. The wheel step is now the `useWheelStep` hook, and `VolumeControl` uses it. `TextInput`, `NumberInput`, `Select`, `Checkbox`, `Slider` and `ToggleGroup` take `mixed` and show Mixed until a new value is set; `NumberInput` takes `onStep`. `Toolbar` groups take `display` menu or slot, items take `openOn` hold with `holdDelay` and `dwell`, a drag from a held menu picks on release, `popover` items open a panel, items take `badge` and `borrowed`, separators take a `label`, a number item draws a `NumberField`, and `keyBadges` draws the key on the tools of a vertical bar, off by default. `inlineOptions` opens the options of the pressed item under its row in a list.
- `useSelectionModel` is the new picking kit in `composites/selection`, taken out of the row selection of `DataTable`, which behaves as before. It takes the keys in display order and the selected set, and gives `click`, `toggle`, `extend`, `step`, `checkAll`, `selectAll`, `clear`, `allState` and an `onKeyDown` for Ctrl and A, Space, Shift with Space, Escape and type ahead. With `cellOf` Shift takes the rectangle between two cells, and `isHole` skips keys that cannot be picked. The helpers `rectKeys`, `toggledKey` and `typeAheadIndex` come with it. `VirtualGrid` takes `selection`, `holes`, `getLabel` and `reorder`. A drag moves the picked tiles with an insertion line, edge scroll and a polite announcement, Alt with an arrow moves them by key, and a section with `onDrop` takes tiles on its header with an into mark. `VirtualList` takes `orientation` set to horizontal with `itemSize`, `selection`, `getLabel`, and a `ref` with `scrollToIndex` and `getRects` for a marquee. The `virtual` kit gains `readHorizontalMetrics`, `stepVirtualSize` and an `axis` for `useVirtualFocus`. `useReorderDrag` takes `keys` for a set, `scrollRef` for edge scroll and `onDrop(from, to, drop)` with the keys, the gap and the modifier held. It returns `dragKeys` and `point`. `moveSet`, `stepGap` and `modifierOf` are new, and `DragGhost` draws a count with up to three stacked thumbnails at the pointer. `useMarqueeSelect` returns `cells`, the bounds of the cells hit, from rects that carry a `cell`, and `hitItems` and `cellRectOf` are exported.
