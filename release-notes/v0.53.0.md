# Tessera v0.53.0

One paragraph for the people who use Tessera: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- ReorderList and SwatchGrid are removed: a layers list and a palette are the Bxel app's own, and it builds them from Tessera's parts. The drag and keyboard reorder logic they shared is now public: `useReorderDrag`, `useMoveFocus`, `reorderStep`, `columnsOf` and `REORDER_ITEM_SELECTOR`, with their types. Build such lists on those hooks, `ColorSwatch`, `ListItemRow` and `IconButton`.
- Card takes `fill`, which makes its body take the card height left under the header and scroll when the child is taller. FactsPanel takes `framed`, and `framed={false}` drops the border, background and padding so it sits inside a Card.
- Adds the checkerboard tokens --c-checker-a, --c-checker-b and --checker-size and the grid line token --c-grid-line, and ColorSwatch uses them for its transparent checkerboard. PixelViewport, merged earlier and never released, is removed; it lives in the Bxel app.
- DataTable takes `defaultExpanded`, a list of group keys or a function, to choose which groups start open. It draws only the rows in view from `virtualizeFrom` rows (200 by default), so a group of hundreds of rows stays fast.
- New composite DocumentTabs. It is the strip of open documents above an editor, such as the drawings of a pixel art editor. Each tab has a title cut with an ellipsis, an optional icon, a hover text, an unsaved dot that turns into the close button on hover or focus, and a close button named Close and the title. A middle click closes a tab. The user puts the tabs in order by dragging or with Alt and Left or Right, through the shared `src/composites/reorder` folder. The strip is a tablist: Left and Right move focus, Home and End go to the ends, Enter or Space activates, and Delete or Ctrl and W asks to close the focused tab. The app keeps `tabs` and `activeId` and can refuse a close in `onClose`. When the tabs do not fit, the strip scrolls with the active tab kept in view, and `overflowMenu` adds a DropdownMenu that lists every tab. Exports: `DocumentTabs`, `DocumentTab` and `DocumentTabsProps`. ScrollStrip takes a new optional `trackProps`, the ARIA attributes of its track, so a strip can name its tablist.
- Exports ownerWindowOf, ownerDocumentOf and observeResize from the primitives path, and adds the focus-ring.css and visually-hidden.css entry points. Apps no longer copy these helpers locally.
- SettingsRow and the content rows of SettingsSection take an optional `hint`. A missing, empty or blank hint draws no hint line, so the row keeps no room for it.
- WindowTitleBar actions take `disabled`, which makes the button and its main menu row inert. `disabledReason` replaces the tooltip text while the action is disabled.
- New icons `eraser`, `paint-bucket`, `pipette` and `hand` for the tools of a drawing editor. They are the Lucide icons of the same names, in the interface set.

## Fixes

- `ControllerSilhouette` redraws its `offset`, `symmetric` and `nintendo` outlines. Each is still one closed line with no fill, mirrored from one half, in the same `0 0 400 260` viewBox. The curves are smoother, with no kinks or wobbles, and the grips, shoulders and the notch between the grips follow the new references more closely. `pad` is unchanged. `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_PATHS` follows the new drawings, and `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_ANCHORS` moves each control of those three variants to where it sits on the reference pad, so an app that places marks from the anchors needs no change. The three outlines are drawn by hand with reference to Noun Project icons: [XBOX Controller](https://thenounproject.com/icon/xbox-controller-5652517/) by Daniel Finnegan, [pro controller](https://thenounproject.com/icon/pro-controller-5602909/) by Eliran Bensadoun and [playstation controller](https://thenounproject.com/icon/playstation-controller-1751312/) by dinnerchief. No path data is taken from them.
- ColorSwatch captions now use a dark ink on light swatch colours and keep the light ink on dark and transparent ones.
