# Tessera v0.53.0

This release keeps Tessera to the foundation every app shares. The layers list and the palette grid move into the Bxel app, and the reorder logic they used becomes public so any app can build such lists. It adds a tab strip for open documents, new tool icons, disabled title bar buttons and long tables that stay fast. It also smooths the controller outlines and makes swatch labels readable on light colours.

## New

- DocumentTabs is the strip of open documents above an editor. Each tab has a title, an optional icon, an unsaved dot that turns into a close button, and a middle click to close. The user orders tabs by dragging or with Alt and Left or Right. Left, Right, Home and End move focus, and Delete or Ctrl and W asks to close. The app keeps `tabs` and `activeId` and can refuse a close in `onClose`. When the tabs do not fit, the strip scrolls with the active tab in view, and `overflowMenu` adds a menu of every tab.
- The reorder logic is public: `useReorderDrag`, `useMoveFocus`, `reorderStep`, `columnsOf` and `REORDER_ITEM_SELECTOR`, with their types. Build any reorderable list or grid on them.
- New icons `eraser`, `paint-bucket`, `pipette` and `hand` for the tools of a drawing editor.
- New tokens for see-through areas: `--c-checker-a`, `--c-checker-b`, `--checker-size` and the grid line colour `--c-grid-line`.
- `ownerWindowOf`, `ownerDocumentOf` and `observeResize` are exported from the primitives path. The `focus-ring.css` and `visually-hidden.css` entry points carry `.focus-ring-inset` and `.visually-hidden`.

## Changes

- DataTable takes `defaultExpanded`, a list of group keys or a function, to choose which groups start open. From `virtualizeFrom` rows (200 by default) it draws only the rows in view, so a group of hundreds of rows stays fast. A focused row stays drawn while it holds focus.
- WindowTitleBar actions take `disabled`, which makes the button and its menu row inert. `disabledReason` replaces the tooltip text while the action is disabled.
- SettingsRow and the content rows of SettingsSection take an optional `hint`. With no hint the row draws no hint line and keeps no room for it.
- Card takes `fill`, which lets the body take the height left under the header and scroll when its child is taller.
- FactsPanel takes `framed`. With `framed={false}` it drops its border, background and padding so it sits inside a Card without a second box.
- ScrollStrip takes an optional `trackProps`, so a strip can name its track.

## Upgrading

- ReorderList and SwatchGrid are removed. A layers list and a palette belong to the app that needs them. Build them on the reorder hooks, ColorSwatch, ListItemRow and IconButton. MIGRATION.md entry 273 has the details.

## Fixes

- ControllerSilhouette redraws its `offset`, `symmetric` and `nintendo` outlines. Each is still one closed line with no fill, in the same `0 0 400 260` viewBox, with smoother curves that follow the reference pads more closely. `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_PATHS` and `CONTROLLER_SILHOUETTE_ANCHORS` follow the new drawings, so marks placed from the anchors need no change. The outlines are drawn by hand with reference to Noun Project icons: [XBOX Controller](https://thenounproject.com/icon/xbox-controller-5652517/) by Daniel Finnegan, [pro controller](https://thenounproject.com/icon/pro-controller-5602909/) by Eliran Bensadoun and [playstation controller](https://thenounproject.com/icon/playstation-controller-1751312/) by dinnerchief. No path data is taken from them.
- ColorSwatch captions use a dark ink on light colours and keep the light ink on dark and transparent ones. The selected, range and focus rings now sit inside the swatch, so they no longer overlap their neighbours.
