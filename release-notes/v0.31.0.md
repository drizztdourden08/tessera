# Tessera v0.31.0

Every menu that opens from a button now opens toward the side with room, so a menu near the right edge of a window keeps its full width instead of being squeezed and cut off.

## Changes

- A menu whose button sits in the right half of the window now opens under the end of its button and spreads to the left at its full width. A button in the left half opens its menu as before.
- The More menus of ActionBar and ItemCard, the row menus of RowGrid, and every other DropdownMenu with a button get this without any change in your app.

## Upgrading

- If one of your menus must always open from a fixed edge, pass `align="start"` or `align="end"` to DropdownMenu.
- MIGRATION.md section 220 describes the change.
