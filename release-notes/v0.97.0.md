# Tessera v0.97.0

This release gives Argus its new shield logo with an emerald twin, optional slots in DynamicInput patterns, pointer based drag and drop on Breadcrumbs, and a right click and focus only keys on RampStrip.

## New

- The Argus mark is a faceted stone shield with eye cutouts and an emerald corner, drawn at three detail levels. `BrandMark` picks the small cut at `sm`, the medium cut at `md`, `h26` and `h39`, and the full cut above. `BrandMarkData.detail` holds the medium and small cuts.
- `BrandMark`, `Logo` and `Logo.Combined` take `twin`, which draws the emerald twin of Argus with the same facets. The tokens `--brand-argus-emerald`, `--brand-argus-stone-*` and `--brand-argus-twin-*` are new.
- A `DynamicInput` pattern slot may be optional: write a `?` after the type, such as `{seconds:number? 1..3600}`. It may stay empty, its placeholder is fainter, and `patternText` drops it with the space before it.
- A `CommandInput` pattern spec is ready with an optional slot empty. `format(values)` gets that slot as undefined, or an empty string for a text slot.
- `Breadcrumbs` serves pointer based drag and drop. `onSegmentRef(id, element)` hands out the element of each segment. `dropOverId` draws the drop look on a segment and runs the spring open hold for it. `springOpen` picks `menu` or `navigate` for what the hold does, and `onSpringOpen(id)` is called either way.
- `RampStrip` takes `onStepContextMenu(index, event)`, called on a right click or the menu key on a step. With `swatchMenu` as well, the handler runs first and the menu opens unless it calls `event.preventDefault()`.

## Changes

- With `selectionKeys` false, the arrow, Home and End keys on `RampStrip` move focus between the steps without picking, and Enter or Space press the focused step.
- `CommandFieldsSpec` declares `setup?: undefined`, so `spec.setup` reads on every member of the `CommandSpec` union.
