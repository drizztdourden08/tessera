# Tessera v0.49.0

A window with no title row can now show its name, and Stepper gains three options for compact ladders. Bare ScreenWindow cards show their title in a small chip at the top left corner, and Stepper can put pictures under the dot, hide its labels and draw at a smaller size.

## New

- A ScreenWindow with no title row shows its title in a small chip at the top left corner, on the same line as the round X. The chip has the height, surface, border and shadow of the corner buttons, small dim text and an ellipsis for a long title. The chip text names the dialog.
- ScreenWindow takes an `icon` prop for a 14 px glyph before the corner title.
- Stepper takes `mediaPlacement`. `mediaPlacement="below"` puts the `media` of each step under its dot, in the column of that step.
- Stepper `labelPlacement` takes `hidden`. The name of each step leaves the view and stays for screen readers, with no label row.
- Stepper takes `size`. `size="sm"` draws a smaller dot, a smaller number inside it and a tighter gap between steps.

## Upgrading

- Every bare ScreenWindow now shows its corner title. Pass `cornerTitle={false}` where something else already names the window, such as a floating switch.
- Stepper `compact` is unchanged and still draws the separate compact view.
