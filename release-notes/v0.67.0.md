# Tessera v0.67.0

This release fixes the widget corner handles from 0.66 and keeps menus inside the window. It also adds widget icons, a plain text clamp, and row drag, folder actions and box selection to DataTable.

## New

- DataTable takes `groupActions` for icon buttons on a group header, `onRowMove(fromKey, target)` for a grip that reorders rows by drag or by Up and Down, and `rowDragData` to drag rows out to an app drop target.
- The DataTable move target gives the shown index, the keys of the rows the drop lands between and the group it lands in, so it maps to the data in a grouped or sorted table.
- A `ref` on DataTable gives `getRects()`, with a rect for every row in the layout, so `useMarqueeSelect` can pick rows of a windowed table. Rows in collapsed groups are left out. `useReorderDrag` takes an optional `locate`.
- WidgetDefinition and WidgetTab take an optional `icon`, drawn before the label in the widget title bar and tab chip.
- FoldText takes `overflow="clip"`: it clamps to its lines with no More control and no hint mark. FormRow and SettingsRow pass it as `descriptionOverflow`.
- Toolbar takes `menuSide`, and ToolbarDock hands it over, so a left bar opens its menus to the right and a right bar to the left.

## Fixes

- DockLayout corner handles are a 24 px square at the panel corner instead of a strip along the whole edge, so they no longer cover buttons and swatches in the panel. Between stacked panels the target sits just above the seam, clear of the title bar buttons below, whatever their number.
- The widget title bar buttons are 24 px across, so they pass the axe target size check. The title bar height and icon sizes stay the same.
- Anchored popups, and so every DropdownMenu, flip to the other side of their button and then stay inside the window with a small margin on all four edges.
- Menus cap their width and wrap long item descriptions, so they never scroll sideways.
- DocumentTabs keeps the unsaved dot visible beside the close button when a tab is hovered or focused.
