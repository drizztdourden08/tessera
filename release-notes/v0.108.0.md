# Tessera v0.108.0

A framed MarkdownEditor and a better fitting avatar mark in SignalStrip.

## Changes

- MarkdownEditor uses the family Toolbar composite. Toolbar, text area and preview sit in one frame with one border, and the frame turns red on an error.
- MarkdownEditor takes `actions`, a row under the frame aligned to the right. The counter sits on the left of that row.
- MarkdownEditor shortcuts show as Ctrl+B or Cmd+B in the toolbar hints.
- The SignalStrip avatar mark is 14 px and its owner ring is drawn flush, so the whole mark is 16 px like a brand mark.
