# Tessera v0.65.0

This release finishes the new toolbar dock. A toolbar can take a corner from the lane beside it, and a dropped toolbar lands where you point. It also finishes the compact card tooltip fix.

## New

- A toolbar end handle dragged past its lane end takes the corner from the lanes it crosses. Dragging back gives it back. The keyboard does the same with one more arrow press at the lane end, and each change is announced.
- A toolbar dropped on a lane lands at the pointer, snapped to the lane and neighbour ends within 8 pixels, and pushes its neighbours along. If even its minimum size does not fit, the drop is refused.
- In keyboard pick mode, PageUp and PageDown step through the slots of a lane, and Home and End go to the first and last slot.
- New stories: two toolbars in one lane, a live ToolbarDock playground, and a DockLayout panel that has taken a corner.

## Changes

- The drop target and the keyboard pick of ToolbarDock have a new shape. MIGRATION 277 covers them and the whole v2 layout.

## Fixes

- In a compact ItemCard, a Tooltip detail that comes after a plain detail, such as a version, now takes the rest of the line and ends in an ellipsis. Before, it overflowed and vanished.
