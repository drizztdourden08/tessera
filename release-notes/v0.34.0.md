# Tessera v0.34.0

Three new parts that any app can use: star ratings that show and pick a score, a carousel for slides, and a viewer that opens a picture or a video large with zoom.

## New

- Rating shows a score to the nearest half star, with the number and the count, and with `onChange` it lets people pick a score with one Tab stop and the arrow keys.
- Carousel shows one slide at a time with dots and arrows. It can turn by itself with `turnEvery`, has a stop button, and never turns while someone is pointing at it, typing in it, looking away or asking for less motion.
- MediaViewer opens a picture or a video large, with zoom up to 1600%, fit or actual size, dragging to look around, sharp pixel art, and the arrow keys to move through a set.
- The icon set gains zoom-in and zoom-out.
