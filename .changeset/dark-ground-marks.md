Every brand mark has a look for a dark ground: `ground="dark"` on `BrandMark`, `Logo` and `Logo.Combined` draws each path in its `onDark` colour and adds the mark's `onDarkRim` as a fine rim. `Splash` gives its marks the dark ground by itself, and `pnpm icons` writes the look as `brand/dark-ground/<app>.svg` and a mark PNG ladder for a static splash page. Brock's stone turns lighter with a fine light rim, Archipelia's ring turns lilac, and Relic of the Past's outline turns bronze. The splash contrast test holds every shape of every mark at 3:1 on its palette's dark gradient.
