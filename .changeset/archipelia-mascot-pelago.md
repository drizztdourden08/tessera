Archipelia has a mascot: Pelago, three purple spheres that press together and drift apart, never still, with floating eyes and floating hands that follow a beat late, over a ring of dots. It has every animation Sentri has (idle, move, jump, wave, look around, happy, alert), and ChosenMascot picks it by the name pelago, by brand="archipelia" or inside data-palette="archipelia". MascotMotion takes ambient, a loop that runs under every clip; MotionTrack takes lag; a scene group takes goo to melt its shapes into one; Svg adds SvgFilter, SvgFeGaussianBlur and SvgFeColorMatrix.
