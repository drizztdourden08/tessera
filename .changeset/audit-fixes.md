`Drawer` closes on Escape through `onClose`, moves focus in when it opens, keeps Tab inside while open and gives focus back to its opener when it closes, with the focus and Escape code of `DialogShell`; it is `inert` while closed. The name of a `FormRow` is a `label` for its control, so a click on it focuses the control or flips a `Toggle`, and focuses the first stop of a group control. `ErrorBoundary` is exported from `@drizztdourden08/tessera/primitives` only, no longer from `/composites`; the root import is unchanged.
