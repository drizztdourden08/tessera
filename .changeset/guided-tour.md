`GuidedTour` and `useGuidedTour` are new: a tour of a screen written as steps, each lighting one part while the rest dims and blurs through `Overlay`, with a rounded glowing hole that glides between targets, a bubble placed with `Anchored`, `onEnter` for the app to change its state, click steps, the arrow keys, Enter and Escape listed in `tour.shortcuts`, and the mascot walking on `MascotStage` to present each step.
