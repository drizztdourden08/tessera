`AnimatedMascot` animates an app's mascot from its pieces with the Web Animations API, typed so each brand takes only its own animation names. Sentri gets Idle, Move, Jump, Wave, Look around, Happy and Alert, each with play and pause, speed and loop, and a still pose under reduced motion. The brand data's new `motion` field lists a mascot's parts, pivots and animations. See MIGRATION.md.
