Every logo takes `rim`: `'none'`, `'light'` or `'dark'`, a thin outline that follows the mark so it reads on dark and light surfaces. The rim colours and widths are tokens, and `pnpm icons` writes every brand's icon files again with each rim under `brand/light-rim/` and `brand/dark-rim/`, always without a tile. See MIGRATION.md and brand/LOGO.md.
