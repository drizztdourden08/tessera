Bxel, the pixel art editor, joins the family as a brand. The `bxel` palette is new: import `@drizztdourden08/tessera/palettes/bxel.css` and set `data-palette="bxel"`. Violet is the primary colour, crimson the secondary, magenta the tertiary, with Night as the black and Mist as the white. `BrandApp` takes `bxel`, and `BRAND_FAMILY.bxel` carries its mark, wordmark, gradient and backdrop. The mark is the Merge 1 logo drawn as paths, and it takes a dark outline on a light ground. The icon files are written under `brand/bxel` and the ground and rim folders, and the Bxel gradient and backdrop tokens are in `brand.css`. Bxel appears in the brand pages and in the app switcher of the gallery. It has no mascot yet.
