`Button` and `IconButton` at `size="xs"`, and a `Tag` you can select, now pass the axe target size check when they sit close to another control, in a short panel or when a row wraps. The control is a 24 by 24 px box (24 px high for a labelled `Button` and a `Tag`) with a 2 px transparent gutter, and it takes no more room than before: a negative margin of the same size keeps the layout, and the border, fill, hover, active, focus and tone rings are drawn on the old 20 px box, so the look is unchanged. The gutter is the new token `--hit-gutter`, `(--hit-area-min - --control-h-xs) / 2`. Pixel look buttons, pixel icons, the large `Tag` and the children of a `ButtonGroup` or `SplitButton` are left as they were, and the Widget title bar buttons keep the 24 px they already drew. The `Toolbar` and `ActionBar` measure rows count the 20 px layout width. The xs tools of an `ActionTile` and the end of a `DocumentTabs` tab sit 4 px apart (2 px before) so their boxes do not overlap. `tokens.css` and `splash-tokens.css` are regenerated with the new tokens `--hit-gutter` and `--hit-gutter-none`.
