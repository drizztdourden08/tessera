`PixelViewport` shows a pixel image of `width` by `height` pixels on a canvas that the user zooms and pans. The app paints the pixels, with `draw(ctx, view)` in image pixel units or with a `source` such as an ImageData or an ImageBitmap, and the viewport owns the view. Pixels stay sharp at any zoom and screen density, over a checkerboard in screen pixels, with a pixel grid from `gridFrom`. The wheel or a pinch zooms around the pointer by whole steps from 1x to 64x, set by `zoomSteps`, and below 1x it halves down to the fit; `wheel="pan"` makes the plain wheel scroll instead. The middle button, Space and drag, Shift and the wheel, and the arrow keys pan, and the image always keeps a margin on screen. Plus and minus zoom around the centre, 0 fits and 1 shows 100%, and the zoom level is read out politely once it settles. `zoom` and `pan` can be held by the app with `onZoomChange` and `onPanChange`; left out, the image fits on mount. `onPointerPixel` reports the image pixel under the pointer, or null outside it. The ref gives `screenToPixel`, `pixelToScreen`, `fit`, `setZoom`, `zoomIn`, `zoomOut`, `redraw` and `view`. New tokens: `--c-pixel-check-a`, `--c-pixel-check-b`, `--c-pixel-grid`, `--pixel-viewport-check`, `--pixel-viewport-margin` and `--pixel-viewport-step`.
