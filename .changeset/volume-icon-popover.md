A volume icon that morphs with the level, a volume slider in a popover, and a volume action on the title bar. `VolumeIcon` (primitives) takes `{ level: number; muted?: boolean; size?: number; className?: string }`: gold waves in `--c-volume-wave` grow from none to three as `level` rises from 0 to 1, and at 0 or muted they fade out as a red X draws in, over `--duration-volume-morph` on transform, opacity and stroke offset only; reduced motion switches at once. VolumeControl and the Video bar draw it. VolumeControl takes `slider?: 'inline' | 'popover'` and `orientation?: 'vertical' | 'horizontal'`: with `popover` it is an icon button that mutes on a click and opens a slider on hover or keyboard focus, which the wheel and the arrow keys move and which closes on pointer leave, blur, Escape or a click away. Its button glyph is now 14 px at md and 12 px at sm. Slider takes `orientation` on a single thumb. DropdownMenu items take `kind: 'slider'` with `value`, `min`, `max`, `step`, `formatValue` and `onChange`, reached with Up and Down and moved with Left and Right. WindowTitleBar actions take `bar: 'volume'` with `{ id, label, value, muted, onChange, onMutedChange, min?, max?, step?, shortcut? }`, drawn as VolumeControl at the bar button size and listed in the main menu as a Mute or Unmute row and a slider row.
