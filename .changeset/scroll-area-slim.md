ScrollArea takes `scrollbar="slim"`: a thin line in the primary colour, painted along the edge so it takes no layout width, wider under the pointer and draggable. SideNav scrolls its groups with it, so the collapsed icon column is no longer squeezed by the scrollbar. New tokens `--scrollbar-slim` and `--scrollbar-slim-active`. See MIGRATION.md.
