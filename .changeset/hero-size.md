Hero takes `size`, `compact`, `default` or `tall`, so an app sets how tall it is. `default` keeps today's look. `compact` fits the content with tighter padding and gaps, for a home with a few facts and actions. `tall` grows to 80 percent of the window height, between 408 and 560 px. Each size stacks under 720 px wide as before. The `HeroSize` type is exported, and the token `--hero-h-tall` is new.
