Adds `KanbanBoard`, a board of cards in app named columns that the user drags within a column or to another one, or moves with the arrow keys on a card grip, reporting each move to the app through `onMove`. Each column shows a count and folds to a narrow rail, `renderCard` draws the inside of a card, and `moveCard` applies a move to the columns.
