Button takes `size="lg"` (56 px, with the pixel look scaled up), `fill="solid"` for a bright call to action, and `caption` for a smaller second line under the label.
With `href` (plus `target`, `rel` and `download`) it renders a link that looks the same, and a disabled or loading one drops the href and sets aria-disabled.
