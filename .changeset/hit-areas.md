The `hit-area` class (with `HIT_AREA_CLASS`, `--hit-area-min` and `--hit-area`) grows the hit area of a small control to at least 24 by 24 px without changing its look. The Tag remove button takes clicks on 24 by 24 px around its 16 px glyph, and the SideNav edge toggle on 28 by 28 px.
