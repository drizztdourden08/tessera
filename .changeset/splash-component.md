`Splash` is new: the static splash page drawn from React with the same `splash.css` classes, with a mark, a title, a status line, a detail, a bar that fills or sweeps, actions and the version, for a start, an update or a reconnect. `splash.css` adds `ts-splash--layer`, `ts-mark`, `ts-detail` and `ts-progress--indeterminate` (MIGRATION section 163).
