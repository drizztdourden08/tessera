Toasts keep one queue per app: `toast()` raises from any part and one `ToastStack`, which replaces `ToastContainer`, draws it, caps how many show with `max` and joins a repeated message into one toast with a count. CommandInput takes `commands` and lists the closest ones under the line as the user types, with Tab or Right to complete and Up and Down to move. PathInput and DropZone show the drop target as soon as a file is dragged in. ActionBar's More button matches the height of its buttons, CopyButton xs draws a 12 px icon with a 24 px hit area, and ConfirmIconButton asks with a green check and a quiet cross.
