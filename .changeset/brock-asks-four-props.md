Four props Brock asked for. `Widget` takes `dragRegion`, which marks its title strip as the window drag region and its buttons as clickable, as `WindowTitleBar` does. A `Hero` with no `art` and no `backdrop` fits its content and starts at the top in place of keeping its full height. A `SettingsRow` action takes `loading`, which shows its button busy while the app runs it. `StatRow` takes `size` (`sm`, `md` or `lg`, the `GaugeSize` scale of StatTile and Gauge).
