`TypeMark` gains `tone`, one of `info`, `success`, `warning` or `danger`, and the type `TypeMarkTone` is exported. When set it wins over `color`: the tile takes the dim status colour and the icon the status ink, and the outline variant shows the icon in the status ink (I-100, Argus P-389).
