`Slider` takes `range` for two thumbs and a `[low, high]` value, `stops` for named positions, `keyStep`, `defaultValue`, `name` and `id`, and `labels`: a rule such as `"every 0.5 | {v}x"`, a list of `[value, label]` pairs, or a function. Labels that would overlap thin out. `RangeSlider` and `RangeInput` are removed in its favour. `Textarea` takes `resize`, with `none` to lock its size. See MIGRATION.md.
