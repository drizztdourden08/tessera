LogPanel takes `order="newest-first"`, which shows the latest line at the top, follows the top and jumps to it, and a row takes its own `tone`. Tabs items take `badgeTone` (`neutral`, `warn`, `danger`, `success` or `info`) to tint the count badge like Badge. A number field takes a `unit`, or `units` in the schema config, so DataTable writes 12.4 MB while sort, group and filter keep reading the number.
