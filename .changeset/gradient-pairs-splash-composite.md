Every palette, the default one too, sets a light gradient pair beside its dark one, `--p-gradient-light-from` and `--p-gradient-light-to`, read as `--c-gradient-light-from` and `--c-gradient-light-to` and listed in `tokens.json` as `gradientLightFrom` and `gradientLightTo`; the Archipelia and Brock pairs are the ends of their bright brand gradient. The splash keeps the dark pair, and its test checks that every palette sets both pairs and that the splash paints no radial gradient. The Gradients page shows each palette's pairs. `Splash` moves from the primitives to the composites, drawn with `Box`, `Pressable` and an internal image element, with its props, classes and look unchanged; its page and the Static splash page move to Composites · Feedback. The RotP mascot files Sentri and the hookshop are drawn again from their current pieces.
