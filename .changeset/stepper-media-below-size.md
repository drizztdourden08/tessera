Add `mediaPlacement`, hidden labels and `size` to Stepper. `mediaPlacement="below"` puts the `media` of each step under its dot, in the column of that step. `labelPlacement="hidden"` hides the name of each step from view and keeps it for screen readers, with no label row. `size="sm"` draws a smaller dot, a smaller number inside it and a tighter gap between steps. `compact` is unchanged.
