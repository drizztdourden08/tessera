Small text is 12 px: `--text-xs` goes from 10 px to 12 px, so captions, hints, tags, badges, mono StatRow values, keycaps and every other small label read more easily (Archipelia review T-03, ux-59). Field labels and Text `variant="label"` no longer force upper case or wide letter spacing; section headers, widget titles, StatTile labels and the overline variant keep their capitals. A small TagInput keeps its entry 80 px wide, so a compact settings row with two tags stays on one line, and ShortcutList keeps a `+` between keys on one line.
