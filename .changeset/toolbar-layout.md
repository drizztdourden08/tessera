Adds the toolbar layout model for the coming ToolbarDock: a JSON-safe `ToolbarLayout` with at most two toolbars per side. Pure ops create, migrate, move, hide, show and toggle it: `createToolbarLayout`, `migrateToolbarLayout`, `moveToolbar`, `hideToolbar`, `showToolbar`, `toggleToolbar`, `isToolbarShown`, `canShowToolbar` and `toolbarOrientation`.
