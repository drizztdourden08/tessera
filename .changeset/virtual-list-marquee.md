VirtualList draws only the rows in view of a very long list of equal rows, with the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down, and the windowing it shares with DataTable is now the useVirtualWindow hook. useMarqueeSelect lets the user drag a rectangle over an area to pick the items inside, with Shift to add, Ctrl or Cmd to toggle, Escape to cancel and scroll near the edges, and MarqueeBox draws the rectangle.
