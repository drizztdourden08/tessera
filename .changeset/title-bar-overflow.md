`WindowTitleBar` takes `actions`, declared once, which show as bar buttons or status pills and as menu items; `left` is removed. Everything the bar shows is also in its hamburger menu: the pin and full screen as check items in a View sub-menu, each action as an item with its status as the subtitle. As the bar narrows, its items hide one by one before the brand gives way, and the logo shrinks before it goes. See MIGRATION.md and RENAMES.json.
