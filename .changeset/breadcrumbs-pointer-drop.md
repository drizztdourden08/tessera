Breadcrumbs serves pointer based drag and drop. `onSegmentRef(id, element)` hands out the element of each segment, `dropOverId` draws the drop look on a segment and runs the spring open hold for it, `springOpen` picks `menu` or `navigate` for what the hold does, and `onSpringOpen(id)` is called either way (I-90, Bxel P-377).
