`ShortcutList` is new: keys, clicks and drags in one column and what each does in the next, every description starting at one edge and every key on the first line of its text, gestures drawn as caps with an icon and a short verb, groups under small headings, and rows that stack when the text column would get too narrow. The WidgetOptions Shortcuts sub-panel and the WindowGuideOverlay rows use it, and the widget title bar loses its long native tooltip.
