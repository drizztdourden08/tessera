Add the token `--c-primary-text`, the primary colour as used for text. It is the bright primary by default, a palette may set `--p-primary-text` to override it, and it reaches 4.5:1 on the background, panel, surface and sunken grounds of every palette.

Small text in the primary tone (the Slider value, StatTile and StatusBar values, Status, Link hover, Shortcut, Toggle link, Widget tab, listbox picked option and more) now uses it, so it turns a little lighter where the seed was too dark.
