A card head row can now be one 16 px line without local CSS: `IconButton` gains size `2xs` (a 16 px square, token `--control-h-2xs`, with a 24 px hit area), `Text` gains `leading="16"` (token `--leading-16`), and `Icon` gains `hue`, which colours the glyph with a category colour of `Tag` (I-92).
