ListItemList takes a `header` with `icon`, `main`, `columns` and `action` labels, drawn as a sticky row on the same tracks as the rows and hidden from assistive technology. A click on a button, link, input or Tooltip trigger inside a DataTable cell no longer selects the row, and `data-cell-control` marks any other element the row should leave alone.
