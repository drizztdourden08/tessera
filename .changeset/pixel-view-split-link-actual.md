Two new pixel icons for a split view: `pixel-view-split-link` shows the two halves joined by a chain, so they pan and zoom together, and `pixel-view-split-actual` shows a 1 in each half, for the view at 100 percent. They sit with the other split view icons and appear in the pixel icon gallery.
