ScreenWindow with no title row now shows its title in a small chip on the top left corner, on the same line as the round X. The chip has the height, surface, border and shadow of the corner buttons, small dim text and an ellipsis for a long title. Pass the new icon prop for a 14 px glyph before the title, and cornerTitle={false} to keep the corner bare. The chip text names the dialog.
