VirtualList takes row heights by index (`itemSize` as a function, `itemSizeKey`), `endGutter`, `renderOption`, `current`, `ownsKey` and `reorder` with thumbnails, `canDrop` and `dropLabel`. VirtualGrid takes `density="swatch"` with `size-16` and `size-20`, `trailing`, `onWheelSize` and `ownsKey`. Navigator takes `overlays`, `onOverlayPress`, `onWheelZoom`, `onKey`, `empty`, `caption` and `describe` (I-72, Bxel P-348).
