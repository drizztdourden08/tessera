A Toolbar separator takes a `priority`, and the items after it up to the next separator fold into More as one group, under that heading, so a group never splits across the bar and More. Without it the fold is unchanged. New pixel icons `pixel-ink-replace`, `pixel-ink-blend` and `pixel-symmetry-badge`. The Toolbar gets a brush picker story: a popover tool holding a ChoiceGrid of crisp brush cells with an Add from selection button, and `ChoiceGrid` documents the recipe.
