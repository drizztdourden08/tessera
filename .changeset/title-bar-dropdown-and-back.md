WindowTitleBar actions take `bar: 'dropdown'` with `groups`, a title bar button that opens its own menu and becomes a sub-menu of the main menu when the window narrows, and a status action takes `pulse`, a pulsing dot on top of its swell and breathing; WindowHeader and ScreenWindow take `onBack` and `backLabel`, a back button before the title; ContentHeader, ScreenPage, SettingsPage and the ScreenWindow header take `back`, a Back to button before the icon that folds to an arrow with a tooltip when narrow. See MIGRATION.md under "Title bar dropdowns, a pulsing status, and back buttons in headers".
