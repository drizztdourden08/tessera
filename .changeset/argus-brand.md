Argus joins the family as a brand. `BrandApp` takes `argus`, and `BRAND_FAMILY.argus` carries its mark, wordmark, gradient and backdrop. The mark is a geometric letter A merged with a watchful eye, drawn in one slate grey until Argus picks a hue. It is a straight app icon with a light outline on a dark ground. The icon files are written under `brand/argus` and the ground and rim folders, the Argus gradient and backdrop tokens are in `brand.css`, and `Hero` takes `data-brand="argus"`. Argus appears in the brand pages and in the brand icons. It has no mascot and no palette yet.
