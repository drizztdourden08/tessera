`Drawer` takes `title`, `subtitle` and `actions`: a `WindowHeader` with a close button at the top, a body that pads its content on every side and scrolls, and an actions row at the bottom. The popups of `DynamicInput` show the Stepper, NumberInput, Slider, option list and ColorPicker in their own look inside one shared frame, and a selected slot draws in the Tessera selection colour in place of the browser's blue box. See MIGRATION.md section 57.
