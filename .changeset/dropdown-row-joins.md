DropdownMenu sub-menus line up with the open row's top or bottom edge before they fall back to the middle, and a join at a menu's first or last row runs straight along that menu's edge. Checkable and radio items keep the menu open when picked; every other item still closes it. Sub-menus place themselves right inside a page scaled with CSS `zoom`.
