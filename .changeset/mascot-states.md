Mascots play 29 clips: the variants idle-bounce, move-wobble, jump-hop, happy-grin and alert-exclaim, the new spin, the expressions default, content, curious, focused, sleep and love, and the interactions working, idea, success, confused, worried, low-power and resting. MASCOT_CLIP_GROUPS sorts them into Motion, Expressions and Interactions and MASCOT_CLIP_VARIANTS names each variant's original. An effect can be fixed to the stage, a clip's still list keeps its symbols in the resting picture for reduced motion, and AnimatedMascot draws only the effects the playing clip uses. Sentri draws every new clip in pixel art.
