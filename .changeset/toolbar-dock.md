Add ToolbarDock, a frame that holds toolbars on the four edges of a work area, up to two lanes on each side. It draws the layout from the toolbar model and hands each toolbar its orientation and a handle for its grip. Dragging the grip moves the toolbar to another lane or side with a drop preview, refuses a full side, and hides it when dropped on the basket. Escape puts it back.
