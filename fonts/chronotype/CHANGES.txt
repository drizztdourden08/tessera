2026-11-04
- Added glyphs for 4 characters the face did not have:
  U+00BC (one quarter), U+00BD (one half), U+00BE (three quarters), U+2605 (black star).
  They are drawn on the face's own pixel grid, and their source outlines are in
  extra-glyphs/.
- Appended "(modified for ChronoKi)" to the version string and a note listing the added
  characters to the description. The credit to Caveras is kept.
- Every original glyph keeps its outline and advance width.
- chronotype-ext.woff2 is the web build and chronotype-ext.ttf the same font as
  TrueType. The optional LTSH and hdmx tables were dropped because they list glyphs by
  number and would no longer match.
